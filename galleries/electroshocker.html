Skin Upgrades

The Electroshocker has eighteen skins. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Twelve skins that are obtained from Crates.
  3. Four skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. One skin that can be obtained from Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The vest from the Level 3+ Default, Valentines, Bunny, Hazmat, and Ghost skins currently wear are the Faraday Vest from R2DA, which, in turn, is named after the scientist Michael Faraday, who studied electrochemistry.
    • The creator of this vest is BelowNatural, who also created Tower Defense Simulator.
  • The Tesla Tower originally used by all skins at Level 2+ was a nod to the Wardenclyffe Tower in Shoreham, Long Island, New York created by Nikola Tesla.
  • The Lightning Cannon used by the Level 4-5 Electroshocker may have been based on the Lightning Cannon from Arsenal.
  • The duck on the level 4-5 Lightning Cannon of the Ducky resembles the Evil Ducky that appeared in D00M's Revenge.
  • The TeeVee skin is based on TV Men from Skibidi Toilet.
    • The Level 4-5 appearance appears to have been based on the Upgraded Titan TV Man.
  • The Jellyfish skin is a submission from the Aquatic Fantasy skin set made by 4it3dm1 and InkEve.
    • The skin originally was a human wearing a jellyfish costume. It was changed into an anthropomorphic jellyfish when it was added into the game.
    • Originally, Level 0-3 would attack enemies using a spell book. This ended up not being used outside of Level 4-5, but even on these Levels it is the jellyfish attacking enemies.
  • According to the Electroshocker Jellyfish Charm, the Jellyfish skin is the result of splicing an Electroshocker tower with a jellyfish.
  • The Lovestriker skin may be a reference to Koharu and Ena from Love Bullet.
  • The Beach skin is non-binary as stated its creator.[1]
  • The model of the Beach was made by mitzu[1] while the concept art was made by petloaches.[2]
  • The Black Cat skin is based on Momo from Magic Cat Academy.[3]
  • The Black Cat skin was made by luigi_star1.[3]

Roblox Catalog

  • The Level 0-4 visor used by the Electroshocker is a re-textured version of the Roblox Visor series from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 1-4 goggles used by the Electroshocker is a re-textured version of the Laser Spec Special Forces Goggles accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The skull seen on the Banned skin is a recolored version of The Riddling Skull from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 0-4 horns seen on the skin are horns from The Dusekkar Roblox item.
  • The Level 5 horns seen on the Banned skin are retextured version of the Flaming Horns series from the Roblox catalog.
  • Legs seen on the Banned skin are retextured legs of Korblox Deathspeaker's package found in Roblox catalog.

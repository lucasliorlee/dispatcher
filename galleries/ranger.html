  1. The Default skin.
  2. Eighteen skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. Six skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. Two skins that can only be purchased through the Daily Skin Store.
  5. Two skins that can be obtained through Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The Wraith skin was made by Kirbylittle.
  • The Partisan skin was made by ingvaras123.
  • The first version of the Default, Green, Blue and Black Ops Level 5 laser rifle was from another game, R2DA. It is known as the FATAL6 which only BelowNatural and R2DA's creator, PlaceRebuilder, have exclusive permission to use.
    • The Ranger's Level 4 upgrade icon still shows the laser rifle.
  • The first and second version of the Default Level 4 cape was loosely based on Star Lord from Guardians of the Galaxy.
  • On the Christmas 2019 Event thumbnail, the Ranger has an appearance that looks like a mix of the original Default skin and Railgunner skin.
    • This appearance was never seen in-game with any skin.
  • The Gun Gale skin is based on Shino Asada from the Sword Art Online Series.
  • The Railgunner skin is based on the Character of the same name from Risk of Rain 2.
    • The Level 4 of the Railgunner also references the Sniper from Risk of Rain Returns.
  • The Classic skin is based on the fourth version of the Default skin.
    • The Classic Ranger does not resemble its BETA version, unlike the Classic Shotgunner, Classic Military Base and John Militant.
  • The Badlands skin is based on the NCR Veteran Ranger from Fallout: New Vegas.
    • The weapons used are also based on weapons from Fallout: New Vegas, with Level 0-3 bearing the Anti-Material Rifle, while the gauss rifle used at Level 4 is YCS/186.
  • The Propellars skin is based on propellars.
  • The 5ouls skin is based on 5ouls.
    • This skin was made as a reward for being one of the first people to triumph Infernal Abyss on Hardcore mode, alongside CrazRex and DanksPlatter. All of them received a skin.
    • The skin was made by 5ouls himself.
  • The Shark skin is a submission from the Aquatic Fantasy skin set made by 4it3dm1 and InkEve.
    • The skin originally used a regular head instead of the current one that resembles a shark far more. Other minor changes, such as colors of certain accessories were also made.
  • The Indigenous skin was formerly called Eskimo Ranger but it was later changed due to the word being considered offensive.
  • The Axolotl skin model was made by 5ouls[1] while the concept art for it was made by colca.[2].
  • The Aquatic skin model was made by Gab7497[3] while its concept art was made by UnknownCanvas.[4]

Roblox Catalog

  • The third and fourth versions of Level 5 Default skin used the Outlaw Hat from the Roblox catalog.
    • This accessory was added to the Roblox catalog way after the current Default Ranger was added.
    • Despite the Ranger's name change from Outlaw to Ranger, the hat name still uses the Outlaw name.
  • The third and fourth versions of Level 5 Black Ops skin used the Dark Outlaw Hat from the Roblox catalog.
    • This accessory was added to the Roblox catalog way after the current Black Ops Ranger was added.
    • Despite the Ranger's name change from Outlaw to Ranger, the hat name still uses the Outlaw name.
  • The original Default, Green, Blue and Black Ops Level 0-2 laser rifle was the Laser Gun of Tomorrow gear from the Roblox catalog.
    • The original Default, Green, Blue and Black Ops Level 3-4 laser rifle was the Super Spy Laser Blaster gear from the Roblox catalog.
      • The Ranger's upgrade icons still show these laser rifles.
  • The first and second versions of the Level 0-1 Default, Green and Blue skins, and the first version of the Level 0-1 Bunny skin, wore the Panspectrum Digital Noobfinders accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The first and second versions of the Level 2-4 Default, Green, Blue and Black Ops skins, and the first version of the Level 2-4 Bunny skin, wore the Optiscope 650 accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The first and second versions of the Level 5 Default, Green, Blue and Black Ops skins, and the first version of the Level 5 Bunny skin, wore the Xenotargeting Oculus accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The first and second versions of Gun Gale skin used the Animazing Hair accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The third and fourth versions of the Dark Matter skin used the Xenotargeting Oculus limited item from the Roblox catalog.
  • Many third and fourth version of the Ranger's skins at Level 5 used a re-textured version of the Great Detective Cape accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The helmet worn by the Badlands skin can be found on the catalog under various versions, with the Arid Badlander Mk.II being the closest to the in-game version.

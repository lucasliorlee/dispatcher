Skin Trivia

  • The default skin of the Jester was originally a skin for the Scout tower which was modified into a full tower.[1]
    • The change from a skin to a tower happened during the development of the first act for the Lunar Overture Event.[2]
    • The various renders of the Jester have a different appearance which is due to this change from a skin to a tower.[3]
      • The background for the v1.9.0 in-game update log and for the Lunar Overture Event season uses an early version of the Lunar Overture Event thumbnail. While bearing many differences, the Jester here is not put in as much focus as in the finished version of it, while also instead of wielding bombs it held something with its arms, which points towards this potentially being the Jester Scout skin.[4]
  • The Beast is based on Portuguese professional footballer Cristiano Ronaldo.
    • The name of the skin is based on the nickname that Spanish sports broadcaster Manolo Lama coined when Cristiano Ronaldo played for Real Madrid, "El Bicho" (literal Spanish for "bug", though it has "creature" connotations)[5].
  • The Flea is based on Argentine professional footballer Lionel Messi.
    • The Flea skin name is based on Lionel Messi's nickname when he played for F.C. Barcelona, "La Pulga" (Spanish for "flea").
      • He is named as such due to his shorter stature compared to other professional footballers.
  • The Flea was a proposed skin that was first speculated after Argentina won the FIFA World Cup 2022 in Qatar.
    • The football kit used by The Beast is the Portuguese national team home kit used during the tournament. Likewise The Flea uses the Argentinian team home kit used during the tournament, except with three stars as opposed to two to symbolize the 2022 triumph.

    • Jester added.

    • Bug: Explosion sounds are not affected by the Towers sound setting.
  • 12 November 2023 - v1.9.0m
    • Updated the explosion visuals for all the bombs, making them unique for each.
    • Bugfix: Second slot bomb icon is now correct.
  • 24 February 2024 - v1.14.0
    • Bug: Jester has no upgrade icons.
    • Bug: Equip Bombs ability uses the Call to Arms ability icon.
  • 1 March 2024 - v1.14.0b
    • Bugfix: Jester's upgrade icons are now present.
  • 7 June 2024 - v1.24.0
    • New skin: Clown
  • 18 September 2024 - v1.32.0
    • Bugfix: Equip Bombs ability now uses the correct icon.
  • 12 February 2025 - v1.53.0
    • New skin: Heartbreak
  • 1 April 2025 - v1.59.0
    • New skin: The Flea, The Beast
    • Bug: The Beast and The Flea skins have broken animations.
    • Bug: Fireworks from the Firework Technician are invisible if generated by the The Beast and The Flea skins.
    • Bug: The Beast and The Flea skins do not stop moving when frozen by both legacy and modern freeze from enemies.
    • Bug: The Beast and The Flea skins' frozen bodies do not have animations.
    • Bug: Stun visuals do not appear above The Beast and The Flea skins' heads.
    • Bug: The Beast and The Flea skins' tower pets have no animations.
    • Bug: Medic Cleansing ability effects do not appear on the The Beast and The Flea skins.
  • 14 May 2025 - v1.64.0
    • Bugfix: Explosion sounds are now affected by the Towers sound setting.
  • 28 May 2025 - v1.65.0
    • Bugfix: Medic Cleansing ability effects now appear on the The Beast and The Flea skins.
  • 18 June 2025 - v1.67.6
    • Bugfix: Stun visuals now appear above The Flea and The Beast skin heads.
    • Bugfix: Fireworks from the Firework Technician generated by The Flea and The Beast skins are no longer invisible.

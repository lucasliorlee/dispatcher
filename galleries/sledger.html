Skin Upgrades

The Sledger has five skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Two skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. One skin obtainable through Mission Quests.
  4. One skin that can be obtained from Crates and Mission Quests.
DefaultBrave SoulPhantomFallenChocolatier
Default
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Pale skin, white arctic camo shirt and pants, black vest, black gloves with grey knuckles, grey shoes and knee-pads, black beanie and a light blue furry fleece collar.

Wields a sledgehammer that is mostly grey, with a blue tip on the near-top to attack enemies.

1 Black balaclava.
2 Black ski goggles with blue visor, black headset and black belt with pouches.

Now wields a maul.

3 Black shoulder-pads, thigh-pads and bag.

Light blue oxygen tank connected to its vest.

4 White artic camo hood.

Larger light blue shoulder-pads and reinforced shoes.

5 Mask with five neon blue lens, ice spikes on shoulder-pads and second oxygen tank.

Now wields a frost maul

The Brave Soul skin is obtained from the Cold Front Crate.

Brave Soul
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Body consists of entirely yellow parts and a floating head to resemble a skeleton.

Light grey chestplate and light grey and dim grey gloves.

Wields a war hammer to attack enemies.

1 Metallic light and dark grey medieval helmet decorated with diamond-shaped bolts.
2 Now wields a spiky war hammer.
3 Custom metallic light and dark grey mask and white bandages.

War hammer now emits frost particles.

4 Light cyan eyes, curled horns attached to the skeleton head and light and dark grey mask with fishnet pattern.
5 Light grey chestplate replaced with an upgraded iron chestplate with light cyan horns.

Red cloth on its chestplate and iron shoulder-pads with light cyan spikes.

Now wields an ice war hammer.

The Phantom skin is obtained from the Phantom Crate.

Phantom
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Black shirts, sleeves, pants, boots and gloves with red straps and white knuckles, grey cybernetic mask with light blue neon divot.

Wields a light blue-greyish mechanic sledgehammer to attack enemies.

1 No change.
2 Now wears a black coat with sleeves. Coat has grey fur on the bottom and the sleeves have red straps with grey lines at the back and at the front.

Wields an upgraded grey mechanic sledgehammer.

3 Upgraded black shoulder pads, red and black frost tanks.

Wields a grey mechanic frost sledgehammer.

4 Coat gains a collar.

Frost tanks now have ice on them.

5 Black knee-pads.

Frost tanks now have two icicles hanging from the bottom.

Wields a black cybernetic frost sledgehammer.

The Fallen skin is obtained through Mission Quests.

Fallen
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Black skin, neon blue eyes, blue and grey armor that consists of chestplate with a neckguard, shoes, gloves and spiked arm-bands, blue shoulder-pads with brown straps and brown belt.

Wields a blue and grey sledge hammer to attack enemies.

1 Grey helmet with a crack in the spot of its right eye hole.
2 Blue crystals around arms.

Now wields a sledge hammer with more crystals on it.

3 Blue and grey tanks attached to the chestplate using dark grey chains.

Blue crystal horns on the helmet.

4 Brown hood with light grey fur.
5 More blue crystals across its body and armor, including the tanks, shoulders and shoes.

Extended chestplate.

Now wields a larger crystal sledge hammer.

The Chocolatier skin is obtained through the Lovestruck Crate or from the Heart Smasher Mission Quest.

Chocolatier
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Tan skin, pink hair, grey shirt with pink accents and grey buttons, grey chef with pink accents, grey gloves, dark purple pants and shoes.

Wields a brown hammer held using a giant grey fork.

1 Grey apron.
2 Grey shades with heart shaped lens.

Grey pouches with pink herats around waist.

Now has a hammer with a bigger hammer head.

3 Dark pink bands around arms.

Large icing gun on back.

4 Pink heart button on chef hat.

Arm bands now have grey buttons.

5 Pink heart button around waist, giant hearts on bands and white scarf.

Recolored clothing, shirt is now gradient dark purple and red, gloves are now dark purple, shades lens are now pink, pouches are now black and pink, shirt buttons are now pink and chef hat is now purple and red, and also taller.

Second icing gun on back.

Head of the hammer is now decorated with various shapes.

Aftershock visuals are multiple sweets left on the ground.

  • The Sledger used the same upgrade icons as the Gladiator before December 20th 2024.
  • The Sledger's Level 4 upgrade icon has a Frozen in it.

Roblox Catalog

  • The Sledger's hood is a retextured version of the Arctic Blue Fuzzy Tiger Hood accessory from the Roblox catalog.

Update History

This only refers to cosmetic changes to the Sledger. Check the main article for statistical changes.

  • 8 May 2021 - Overhaul v1.3.0
    • Sledger added.
  • 17 April 2022 - v1.2.0
    • Default Level 0-5 appearance changed.
  • 14 February 2023 - v1.6.2
    • New skin: Brave Soul
  • 21 December 2023 - v1.11.0
    • New skin: Phantom
  • 21 August 2024 - v1.28.0
    • New skin: Fallen
    • Bug: Fallen skin's left glove has a broken mesh.
  • 23 October 2024 - v1.37.0
    • Bug: Sledger has broken animations.
  • 20 December 2024 - v1.47.0
    • Default Level 0-5 appearance changed.
    • Brave Soul and Fallen skin animations changed.
    • Level 0-5 upgrade icons changed.
    • Bug: Brave Soul and Fallen skins have an outdated icon.
    • Bugfix: Sledger animations now work properly.
  • 5 February 2025 - v1.52.0
    • Bugfix: Fallen skin now has a working left glove mesh.
  • 12 February 2025 - v1.53.0
    • New skin: Chocolatier
  • 21 August 2026 - v2.7.0
    • Changed icons for the Brave Soul and Phantom skins.

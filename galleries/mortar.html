Skin Upgrades

The Mortar has sixteen skins in total. These include:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Eight skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. Five skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. One skin that can be unlocked from Mission Quests.
  5. One skin that can be unlocked from Crates and Mission Quests.

  • The original Level 5 scaffolding went down to ground normally occupied by ground towers.

    • Because of this, the Bunny skin was broken until the Entity System Update.
  • On specific levels, the shells the Mortar drops into the cannon differ from the ones fired out.
    • The levels at which these differ depends on the skin. For most skins, it is for Level 3-4.
  • In the Frost Invasion Event trailer, a Frost Mortar is shown. However, the skin was cancelled.
    • This skin was later added three years after the release of the Frost Invasion Event.
  • On the render seen in the Gift Box after purchasing the Unwavering Tides Bundle gamepass and the icon of the gamepass itself, and the Unwavering Tides Update thumbnail and trailer the Pirate Mortar has a different appearance Level 4 than the one in-game.
    • Most notably, on the render, the Pirate Mortar wears a black and blue coat, while in-game the Pirate Mortar only wears a white shirt.
    • The Pirate Mortar has a cutlass and a scabbard for it in the render, both of these are not present in-game.
    • The Pirate Mortar has black fingerless gloves in the render, while in-game it wears regular brown gloves.
    • On the render, behind the belt, there appears to be red cloth, which is not present in-game.
    • The Pirate Mortar in the render appears to have tan skin, while in-game it has a pale skin.
  • The Pirate and Defender Mortar skins at Level 5 do a laugh after firing, which is Wario's laugh, from Super Smash Brothers: Ultimate.
  • Vigilante, Pirate, Defender, Frost, and Dark Frost skins at Level 5 has the same firing sound as Level 3-4.
  • The Festive skin is a submission made by enbycoated.
    • Originally, the skin was designed to be more human, before being altered to give it a more deer-like appearance.
  • The Beach skin was made by mitzu while its concept art was made by TrustlyDragon.[1]

Roblox Catalog

  • The visor used by the Default, Bunny and Eclipse Mortar is a re-textured version of the 2007 ROBLOX Visor accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 3-5 goggles used by the Default, Bunny and Eclipse Mortar is a re-textured version of the Combat Goggles accessory from the Roblox catalog.

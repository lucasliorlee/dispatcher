Skin Upgrades

The Brawler has fourteen skins in total, those being:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Six skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. Four skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. One skin that can be unlocked from Mission Quests.
  5. One skin that can be unlocked from the Daily Store.
  6. One skin that can be unlocked from Crates and Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The Loader skin is based on Loader from Risk of Rain 2.
  • The Jordan skin is based on the Roblox Content Creator Jordan Stayt with the Level 4 and 5 being based on one of his previous avatars.
    • The cost of the skin is a reference to the 32 meme. It is referenced here due to the myth of it being a clue to activating the Hidden Wave becoming quite popular on one of his livestreams.
  • The Jordan skin has a low chance to emit a glitch effect similar to that of The32 Nametag when landing after using the Reposition ability.
  • The Lovestriker skin is based on Chiyo from Love Bullet.
  • The Lobster skin is a submission from the Aquatic Fantasy skin set made by 4it3dm1 and InkEve.
    • The skin was originally a human wearing a lobster costume. It would be changed into an anthropomorphic lobster when it was eventually added into the game.
    • According to the Brawler Lobster Charm, the Lobster skin is a Brawler tower fused with a lobster's might.
  • The Horse skin is likely based on Special Week, Vodka, and Kitasan Black from Umamusume: Pretty Derby.
  • The Bunny skin's color scheme being similar to the transgender flag is completely unintentional.[1][2]
  • The Scuba Ops skin was made by Linx[3] while its concept art was made by Loonimoff.[4]
  • The Scuba Ops skin is based on the PRAWN Suit from Subnautica.
  • The Beach skin was made by supereroulnoptizet.[5]

Roblox Catalog

  • The Banned skin's skull at Level 5 is the The Ice Skull of Nevermoor.

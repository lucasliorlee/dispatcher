Skin Trivia

  • The early appearance closely resembles the Frost Invasion Update look of the default skin, as both are towers with pale skin wearing a black suit with a black tie and white shirt that wield a luger pistol with their right hand.

Update History

  • 17 July 2026 - v2.3.0
    • Kingpin added.
    • Bug: Kingpin continues to visually attack enemies while frozen or stunned.
    • Bug: When placing a Bounty on an enemy at Level 6A, the Desert Eagles it wields will float in the air.
    • Bug: Kingpin at Level 6B has two sets of pants, making the intended striped pants hard to see.
  • 21 August 2026 - v2.7.0
    • Updated the icon for the Overhaul Update version of the default skin.

Skin Trivia

  • The Liberator skin is based on Helldivers 2.
    • The Level 2 Liberator Rifleman is based on the DP-53 Savior of the Free armor from Helldivers 2.
    • The Level 2 Liberator Grenadier is based on the FS-23 Battle Master armor from Helldivers 2.
    • The Level 1 Liberator Field Medic is based on the B-24 Enforcer armor from Helldivers 2.
    • The Level 1 Liberator Riot Guard is based on the B-27 Fortified Commando armor from Helldivers 2.
    • The space shuttle that air-drops units is the Pelican-1 from Helldivers 2.

Update History

This only refers to cosmetic changes to the Mercenary Base. Check the main article for statistical changes.

  • 19 April 2024 - v1.19.0
    • Mercenary Base added.
    • Bug: Sound of the plane flying by is not affected by the Towers sound volume setting.
  • 18 May 2024 - v1.22.0
    • Level 4 upgrade icon changed.
    • Bug: Mercenary Base tower pet does not update properly when changed.
  • 7 June 2024 - v1.24.0
    • New skin: Liberator
    • Bug: Level 0 Liberator Riot Guard has collision.
  • 2 August 2024 - v1.27.0
    • Updated explosion visual effects.
  • 27 November 2024 - v1.43.0
    • Level 1 and 2 upgrade icons swapped.
  • 16 December 2024 - V1.46.0
    • New skin: Frost Legion
    • Bug: Frost Legion Rifleman, Grenadier, Riot Guard and Field Medic have collision.
    • Bug: Frost Legion Field Medic uses a green shield.
    • Bug: Frost Legion Grenadier has a visible hitbox.
    • Bug: Frost Legion Grenadier Humanoid Rig is visible through the fake limbs.
    • Bug: Frost Legion Grenadier explosion sounds are not affected by the Towers sound volume setting.
    • Bug: Frost Legion skin units have grey cubes on their wings.
  • 8 January 2025 - v1.48.1a
    • Bugfix: Frost Legion Rifleman, Grenadier, Riot Guard and Field Medic no longer have collision.
    • Bugfix: Frost Legion Field Medic no longer uses a green shield.
    • Bugfix: Level 0 Liberator Riot Guard no longer has collisions.
    • Bug: Frost Legion Grenadier and Field Medic have a large, visible grey cube on them when seen in the inventory.
  • 5 February 2025 - v1.52.0
    • Bugfix: Frost Legion Grenadier and Field Medic no longer have a large, visible grey cube on them when seen in the inventory.
  • 12 February 2025 - v1.53.0
    • Bugfix: Frost Legion Grenadier no longer has a visible hitbox.
  • 28 May 2025 - v1.65.0
    • Bug: Frost Legion Grenadier creates large explosions visually if the Improved Gunpowder Skill is active.
  • 18 October 2025 - v1.77.0
    • New skin: Graveyard
    • Bug: Graveyard Rifleman weapon has z-fighting with upgrades.
    • Bugfix: Sound of the plane flying by is now affected by the Towers sound volume setting.
    • Bugfix: Frost Legion Grenadier explosion sounds are now affected by the Towers sound volume setting.
  • 21 August 2026 - v2.7.0
    • Updated icon for the default skin.

Skin Trivia

  • The Gladiator currently has the most skins for an event tower.
  • Currently no Gladiator skins share the same weapon.
  • The Beach skin was originally going to be called the Ducky Gladiator.
    • In a few development images, the Ducky Gladiator had duck-themed cosmetics that were removed when the Beach skin was added to the game.
  • The Slugger is based on the RED Scout from Team Fortress 2.
    • The bat used by the Slugger skin between Level 3-4 is based on the Bat that the Scout uses.
    • The boombox on the back of the Slugger skin may be based on the Boston Boom-Bringer cosmetic item for Scout.
    • The previous Level 5 bat is similar to the Classic Baseball Bat used in R2DA with the Barbed Wire attachment.
    • The design of the bat used on Level 5 is based on the Atomizer bat usable by the Scout.
  • The Pumpkin skin is heavily based on Lord Sinister.
  • The Gladiator previously shared its Level 2 icon with the Archer's previous Level 3 icon.
    • This is strange as the Gladiator did not have a ranged attack.
  • The previous Level 5 Demon skin having red eyes is likely a reference to the first version of the Default Level 5 Gladiator having red eyes.
  • On one of the renders used as background for Leaderboards, the Default skin is depicted to have a blue cape, which is never seen in game.
    • As the render on Roblox is called "VersusGraphic", this is due to it being a reused image for the Versus mode, with cape colors representing the red and blue teams.
  • On the render seen in the Gift Box after purchasing the Unwavering Tides Bundle gamepass, and the Unwavering Tides Update thumbnail and trailer, the Pirate Gladiator has flintlocks on its torso. However, these are not present on the Pirate Gladiator in-game.
  • The Pirate Gladiator skin is a reference to Roronoa Zoro from One Piece.
  • The Galactic skin appearance is based on Sith Lords from Star Wars, though it does not appear to be based on any specific character.
  • The blade used by the Vigilante skin may be a reference to the Mantis Blades from Cyberpunk 2077.
  • The Cameraman skin was originally meant to be released in the April Fools 2024 Update.
  • The Cameraman skin is based on the Cameramen from Skibidi Toilet.
  • The Beach skin's pool noodle make unique squeaking sounds when it hits an enemy, which differs from the other skins.
  • The Phantom skin's katana sheathe is likely heavily inspired by Murasama from Metal Gear Rising: Revengence.

Roblox Catalog

  • The Linked Sword (Level 0), Brigand's Sword (Original Level 2), Venomshank (Original Level 3), Illumina (Level 4) and Darkheart (Level 5) swords, which were used by the Gladiator at one point, are all gears found in the Roblox catalog.
    • These swords are well known in Roblox's history. The Linked Sword was one of the six weapons in BrickBattle. Brigand's Sword was the first gear ever published. The Venomshank, Illumina and Darkheart are unique swords from Sword Fight on the Heights IV.
  • The helmet that the Default skin uses between Level 2-4 is the Trojan Infantry Helmet from the Roblox catalog.
  • Previously, the face used by the Slugger skin was the Fearless face from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 0-1 weapon used by the Slugger skin is the Baseball Bat gear from the Roblox catalog.
    • The Level 2-4 baseball bats used are a re-textured version of it.
  • The boombox on the back of the Level 5 Slugger skin is the Vibecaster Boombox accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The accessory was uploaded by BelowNatural over half a year after the Slugger skin was added to the game.
  • The headset used by the level 5 Slugger skin is the Black Tactical Headset accessory from the Roblox Catalog.
  • The Level 5 baseball cap used by the Slugger skin is the Baseball Cap with Lightning Design accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 2+ shades used by the Slugger skin are the Ice Cold Stunnas from the Roblox Catalog.

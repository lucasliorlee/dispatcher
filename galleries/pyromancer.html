Skin Upgrades

The Pyromancer has twenty skins. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Thirteen skins that are obtained from Crates.
  3. Five skins that are obtained from Passes.
  4. One skin that is obtained from Passes and Crates.
  5. One skin that can be unlocked from the Merch Shop.

Skin Trivia

  • Prior to the Frost Invasion Update, the color of the flames were different with certain skins. For example, the Hazmat and Ghost skins had blue fire, and the Golden skin had yellow fire.
    • The original Platinum and Golden icons were inaccurate as the flamethrower model was different to the one used in the game.
  • The first version of the Hazmat skin was based on the Ticker from R2DA.
  • The design and shape of the Golden (and formerly the Platinum) flamethrower may be inspired by a blowtorch called Not a Flamethrower.
  • The original Default Level 3 upgrade was similar to the appearance of Pyro from Team Fortress 2 as it wore a bandolier with fire grenades.
    • Additionally, at Level 4, the original Default Pyromancer gained a gas tank on its back, which was similar to the Team Fortress 2 variant.
    • The flamethrower currently used by all Levels 0-4 skins seems to be based on the Team Fortress 2 Pyro's Flame Thrower as well, most particularly the nozzle and pilot light.
    • The flamethrower currently used by most Level 5 skins seems to be based on the Flamethrower from R2DA.
  • The original Hazmat icon was its Level 4 appearance without the armbands, gloves and welding mask.
  • The in-game icon for the Pool Party has no face and has a visible humanoidroot connector.
  • The Fire Breather skin is based on a submission made by superbonk06.
  • The Reindeer skin is based on a submission made by linxthedeveloper.
  • The Hallow Punk skin is likely based on Jinx from the TV series Arcane.
  • The Scuba Ops skin is likely based on Ryley Robinson from the game Subnautica.
  • The Scuba Ops skin was made by luigi_star1.[1]

Roblox Catalog

  • The original Level 2-5 welding mask used on most skins is the Welding Mask accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The current Default Level 2-5 gas mask is the Firefighters Breather Amber accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The Default Level 5 gas mask is a re-textured version of it.
    • The Level 2-5 gas mask on most other skins is a re-textured version of it.
    • The original Scarecrow Level 2-5 mask is the Scarecrow Gas Mask from the Roblox catalog.

Skin Upgrades

The Firework Technician has three skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. One skin that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. One skin that can be unlocked from Mission Quests
DefaultInventor2026
Default
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Tan skin, blue shirt and beanie, black pants, red shoes with white laces, white belt with yellow buckle.

Holds a red and white firework which it fires at the beginning of the wave.

1 Red and white vest.
2 White and blue scarf with scarf, yellow and black headset, blue goggles.

White and blue star on vest.

Now holds two additional fireworks, one blue and dark blue, the other white and yellow, firing all three at once.

3 Yellow top hat, brown hair and blue, yellow and white jacket.

Vest replaced with a red and white suit, pants are now white, goggles are now black and red.

4 Now holds a cane.

Jacket is now blue, red and white.

Now fires the fireworks from a large blue, red and yellow cannon.

The Inventor skin is obtained from the Showtime Crate.

Inventor
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Pale skin, white and pink shirt, two brown belts, light pink pants and black shoes.

Holds a pink and red firework which it fires at the beginning of the wave.

1 Brown vest and bag with fireworks.
2 Red and black checker scarf and brown hat.

Now carries two additional fireworks, one yellow and pink and other purple and pink.

3 Purple goggles.
4 Brown coat worn on only one shoulder.

Goggles are now on top of its hat.

Firework bag and multiple fireworks held are replaced by a cane.

There is now a big brown firework cannon it fires fireworks from.

The 2026 skin is obtained from the Mission Quests.

2026
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Dark skin, brown hair, black and yellow vest, blue pants, black and yellow shoes, red belt and black hat with red stripe.

Holds a black and yellow firework which it fires at the beginning of the wave.

1 Red and yellow buckles on torso, yellow stars on sleeves.
2 Yellow shades and yellow stars on hat.

Now holds two additional fireworks, one white and yellow the other yellow and red.

3 Jacket that is yellow on the left with red stars and black on the right with yellow stars.

Hat is now a tophat which is yellow on the left with stars and black on the right with 2026 written there.

4 Yellow details around shoes and pants.

Black parts on the jacket, shirt, tophat and shoes are now red, pants are now white.

Fireworks held are replaced by a cane.

There is now a big red and yellow firework cannon it fires fireworks from.

Update History

    • Firework Technician added.
    • Bug: Default skin's torso is made out of grey plastic.

    • New skin: 2026
    • Bug: 2026 skin's tophat at Level 3 does not disappear when upgraded to Level 4, causing z-fighting with the Level 4 tophat.

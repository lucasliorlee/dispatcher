Skin Upgrades

The Hacker has six skins. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Two skins that can be obtained from Crates.
  3. One skin that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. One skin that can be obtained from Mission Quests.
  5. One skin that could have been obtained from triumphing Hidden Wave.

Skin Trivia

  • The unused default skin appearance was based on Aiden Pearce from WATCH_DOGS.
    • The default skin that did make it into the game does not appear to actually resemble him.
    • The Hacker would also be referred to with he/him[1] pronouns instead of she/her, meaning the gender of the tower also changed prior to release.
  • The Pool Day skin is based on Vocaloid Akita Neru.

Credits

  • The Camera Operator skin is a submission made by kingk02.
    • The skin rig and its animations were done by supereroulnoptizet, profesionally known as linxthedeveloper.
    • The design was conceptualized by miracle2kki.
  • The Pool Day skin is a submission made by enbycoated.[2]

Update History

This only refers to cosmetic changes to the Hacker. Check the main article for statistical changes.

  • 11 June 2025 - v1.67.0
    • Hacker added.
    • Skins: Fallen, Triumphant.
    • Bug: Converted enemies sometimes do not turn into holograms.
    • Bug: Hacker's drone is not affected by Time Scale.
    • Bug: Hacker's default drone creates a collidable part.
    • Bug: When multiple towers can be cloned by the Hacker, the towers will start to no longer be highlighted.
    • Bug: Fallen skin has a floating part.
    • Bug: Fallen skin does not use the placement visual effects other Fallen skins use.
    • Bug: Hacker does not properly turn multiple body parts translucent.
    • Bug: When its soul is stolen by the Void Reaver, its synapse animations break.
    • Bug: Fireworks from the Firework Technician are invisible.
    • Bug: Hacker does not stop moving when frozen by both legacy and modern freeze from enemies.
    • Bug: Hacker's frozen body does not have animations.
    • Bug: Stun visuals do not appear above Hacker's head.
    • Bug: Tower pet has no walking animation.
    • Bug: Triumphant skin hair changes colors at Level 1.
  • 18 June 2025 - v1.67.6
    • Bug: Stun particles appear inside the Fallen/Triumphant Hacker's head at Level 5.
    • Bugfix: Drone is now affected by Time Scale.
    • Bugfix: Stun visuals now appear above the Hacker's head.
    • Bugfix: Fireworks from the Firework Technician are no longer invisible.
  • 20 December 2025 - v1.83.0
    • New skin: Camera Operator
  • 20 December 2025 - v1.83.0a
    • New skin: Reindeer Mech
  • 28 January 2026 - v1.84.0
    • Improved Camera Operator skin beams visuals, they now look like a film reel instead of just using the default effects.
    • Changed Camera Operator skin clone colors, previously used default skin colors.
  • 2 July 2026 - v2.1.7
    • New skin: Pool Day
    • Bug: Pool Day skin drone's beam has a collision hitbox that can fling players.
    • Bug: Pool Day skin's cellphone does not disappear at Level 5, which causes it to be held in one hand alongside a smoothie at Level 5B and at Level 5A, it will be clipping inside the banana tube instead.
    • Bug: Pool Day drone sounds do not play.
  • 5 July 2026 - v2.2.1a
    • Bugfix: Pool Day drone sounds now play.

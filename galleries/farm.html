Skin Upgrades

The Farm has twenty-two skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Nine skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. Two skins that can be unlocked from a Code.
  4. Six skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  5. Two skins that can be unlocked from Crates and Passes.
  6. Two skins that can be obtained through Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The Level 2 Default appearance was based on BelowNatural's wheatfields in the map Ducky's Bootcamp, from Reason 2 Die: Awakening.
    • The original design was taken directly from the model.
  • The Level 0 Xmas appearance was based on the little Christmas tree Charlie Brown adopts from the short Christmas animated television special, A Charlie Brown Christmas.
  • The Tycoon skin was based on buildings commonly found in Roblox tycoon games.
  • Multiple Farm skins change the sound and the cash pop up which appears when the Farm generates money. These changes for each skin are:
    • The Arcade skin changes the font and the cash icon to be pixelated. Changes the sound into an arcade sound.
      • The sound actually came from the Metal Slug video game series.
    • The Xmas and Present skins change the sound into a Christmas bells ringing sound.
    • The Graveyard skin changes the font, the text into dark yellow, and makes the cash icon darker. Changes the sound into a bones falling sound.
      • The sound is actually the same as the death sound of the Bones, Hallow Bones and Hallow Guard.
    • The Ducky skin changes the sound into a quack sound, but high pitched and repeated three times.
    • The Crypto and Pirate skin change the font. The font is different for both skins.
  • In the Frost Invasion Event trailer, a scene shows the Farm with a Vendor skin. However, the skin was initially cancelled due to the skin being broken.
    • The skin was added over three years later as a code skin.
  • In the Pizza Party map, there are arcades scattered around the map that bear a resemblance to the Arcade Farm.
  • The car that appears in the Level 4-5 Crypto skin is based on the Tesla Cybertruck.
    • The same car being attached to a rocket is most likely a reference to Elon Musk sending his Tesla Roadster to space.
  • The Pirate skin is the first animated Farm skin.
    • Additionally, the Pirate is larger than the Farm's placement footprint.
  • The Pot Of Gold skin has a unique cash collection effect sending the player green Tix instead of cash.
  • The Present skin is currently the only Farm skin that emits light.
  • The Booth skin has a unique cash collecting feature where it sends the player Robux instead of cash.
  • The Booth skin is the largest skin in the entire game at Level 5.

  • The PNG skin is based on the first version of the Farm, though with the Level 0 removed, which is also the ones used in its upgrade icons.
  • The gargoyles that appear on the Null Soul skin are based on the Gargoyle Commander skin.
  • The Cinema skin features multiple references to real life creations.
    • At Level 1, there is a movie poster for "Exo Strikes Back", a reference to Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back.
    • At Level 2, there is a movie poster for "Attack of the Ducks; A Duck Who Sold the World", which references multiple things:
      • The poster image is a reference to the Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain game release poster.
      • The working poster title, "A Duck Who Sold the World", is a reference to the David Bowie song The Man Who Sold the World, which was later covered by Midge Ure and used in Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain.
      • The prefixed poster title, "Attack of the Ducks", is a reference to Star Wars Episode II: Attack of the Clones.
        • The roman numeral "V" which is behind the prefixed title is another reference to Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain.
    • At Level 3, the movie title "A forgotten God's Dream" is a custom title with no outside reference.
  • According to the Popsicle Vendor skin's concept artist, the skin was supposed to have custom animations, but it was not included due to time constraints.[1]
    • The dog's name in the skin is also Pop.[2]

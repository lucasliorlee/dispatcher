Skin Upgrades

The Necromancer has five skins in total. These include:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. One skin that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. One skin that can be unlocked from Mission Quests.
  4. Two skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
DefaultMageFallenDuckCreepy Santa
Default
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Tan skin, dark grey torso, hood, pants and shoes, dark grey robe with green accents and a brown belt.

Wields a dark brown wooden staff with a skull with grey horns on it and fires magical bolts.

Summons grey gravestones with its staff on the path.

1 Black knuckle gloves.
2 Lantern with glowing blue light on waist.
3 Grey pauldrons and brown cape.

Puts on its hood, shrouding its eyes in darkness.

Unlocks Level 2 gravestones, which are slightly larger and have a crack in them.

4 Left arm and leg are now skeletal, along with a part of its torso and head missing, revealing its bones.

Light grey armband and knee-pads with skull marks, donning neon blue eyes on its skeletal limbs.

Hood replaced with a dark grey dominus with green feathers.

Green accents are now neon blue.

Hovers off the ground, constantly convulsing.

Now wields a spell book along with a bigger staff.

Unlocks Level 3 gravestones, which are slightly larger, darker, have a bigger crack, neon blue insides, black skull with neon blue eyes and multiple spikes.

The Mage skin is obtained from the Deluxe Crate.

Mage
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Tan skin, dark green torso, coat and pants, leafy green scarf, dark brown shoes, dark green robe with light green accents, green capillary patterns and a mustard yellow belt.

Wields a brown wooden staff with two glowing green crystals and leaves, which fires magical bolts.

Summons small brown roots with its staff.

1 Black knuckle gloves.
2 Jar with two glowing butterflies, one blue and one yellow, on waist.
3 Green leafy pauldrons, and green hood and tattered cape.

Now has a brown antler growing out of the right side of its head.

Now unlocks Level 2 gravestones, where the root has green leaves and two neon green crystals.

4 Left arm and leg are now carved wood, along with a wooden mask covering half of its face, all of which have glowing green parts.

Scarf now has pink and yellow flowers and a wooden neck brace on it.

Hovers off the ground, constantly convulsing.

Now wields a glowing green sigil along with a bigger staff.

Now unlocks Level 3 gravestones, where the root has claw-like branches which cover the crystals.

Level Image Cosmetic Changes

Wields a staff that looks like reindeer antlers and trows sharp ice crystals from its hands.

Update History

  • 31 October 2023 - v1.9.0g
    • Necromancer added.
    • Bug: Necromancer uses the Call to Arms ability icon.
    • Bug: Necromancer does not have sound effects.
  • 31 October 2023 - v1.9.0h
    • Bugfix: Now uses the correct ability icon.
  • 3 November 2023 - v1.9.0j
    • Bug: Sound of the gravestones being destroyed is not affected by the Towers sound setting.
    • Bugfix: Necromancer now has sounds effects.
  • 12 November 2023 - v1.9.0l
    • New Level 1-4 upgrade icons.
  • 29 March 2024 - v1.17.0
    • New skin: Mage
  • 10 May 2024 - v1.21.0
    • Bug: Hallow Guard animations are not affected by Time Scale.
  • 21 August 2024 - v1.28.0
    • New skin: Fallen
  • 16 April 2025 - v1.61.0
    • New skin: Duck
    • Bug: Duck Skeleton, Sword Skeleton and Skeleton Knight have collisions.
    • Bug: Duck Sword Skeleton and Skeleton Knight float slightly above the path.
    • Bug: None of the Duck skeletons have death animations.
    • Bug: After the Duck Executioner Skeleton throws its blades, they will be stuck in its body upon its return.
    • Bug: Default skeletons use the Duck skin appearances.
    • Bug: Fireworks from the Firework Technician generated by the Duck skin are invisible.
    • Bug: Duck skin does not stop moving when frozen by both legacy and modern freeze from enemies.
    • Bug: Duck skin's frozen body does not have animations.
    • Bug: Stun visuals do not appear above Duck skin's head.
    • Bug: Duck skin tower pet has no walking animations.
    • Bug: Medic Cleansing ability effects do not appear on the Duck skin.
    • Bug: After throwing its axe, all Executioner Skeleton skins except Duck will stop holding it.
  • 18 April 2025 - v1.61.5
    • Bugfix: Default skeletons now use the correct appearance.
  • 23 April 2025 - v1.62.0
    • Reduced Duck Giant Skeleton size.
    • Increased Duck Executioner Skeleton size.
  • 28 May 2025 - v1.65.0
    • Bugfix: Medic Cleansing ability effects now appear on the Duck skin.
  • 18 June 2025 - v1.67.6
    • Bugfix: Stun visuals now appear above the Duck skin head.
    • Bugfix: Fireworks from the Firework Technician generated by the Duck skin are no longer invisible.
  • 9 July 2025 - v1.70.0
    • Bug: Reverted the size changes made in v1.62.0.
  • 18 October 2025 - v1.77.0
    • Bugfix: Sound of the gravestones being destroyed is now affected by the Towers sound setting.
  • 20 December 2025 - v1.83.0a
    • New skin: Creepy Santa
    • Bug: Fallen Executioner Skeleton has broken attack animations.
  • 21 August 2026 - v2.7.0
    • Updated icons for the default and Mage skins.

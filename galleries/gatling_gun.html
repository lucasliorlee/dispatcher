Skin Upgrades

The Gatling Gun has two skins. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. One skin that can be unlocked from Passes.
DefaultEaster
Default
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Operator is the player's avatar forced to R6 and without any dynamic clothing.

Grey and green M2 Browning with a green ammunition box, on a tripod grey and green mount.

1 No change.
2 M2 Browning is now mostly grey, with a green shield at the front.

Ammunition box is now lighter, lid is now on the side instead of on top of it.

Now on a quadpod mount, which is mostly grey.

3 Double-barreled M2 Browning with two ammunition boxes. Expanded shield now covers both sides of the operator.

Green parts are now light blue.

4 Operator is now seated.

Mount legs are now a stand.

M2 Browning replaced with a turret minigun, covered by more shield.

5 Stand and turret minigun is now mostly covered in green armor.

Operator is protected by a green panel, with a small viewing window at the top.

6 Even more armor plating on the weapon.

Protective panel for operator removed, now has a camera at the front to see enemies with.

Armor is now black.

Turret minigun is now bigger.

The Easter skin was obtained from Level 15 of the Ducky Revenge Battle Pass premium track.

Easter
Level Image Cosmetic Changes
0 Operator is the player's avatar forced to R6 and without any dynamic clothing.

Orange and blue M2 Browning with a blue ammunition box and an orange tip, on a tripod blue white and orange mount.

1 No change.
2 M2 Browning now has a blue shield at the front.

Ammunition box is now lighter, lid is now on the side instead of on top of it.

Now on a quadpod mount, which is blue white and orange.

3 Double-barreled M2 Browning with two ammunition boxes. Expanded shield now covers both sides of the operator.

Blue parts of the shield are now white, blue parts of legs being orange.

4 Operator is now seated.

Mount legs are now a stand.

M2 Browning replaced with a turret minigun, covered by more shield.

Color scheme is now mostly blue with a white stripe.

5 Stand and turret minigun is now mostly covered in blue and orange armor.

Operator is protected by a green panel, with a small viewing window at the top.

6 Even more armor plating on the weapon.

Protective panel for operator removed, now has a camera at the front to see enemies with.

Armor is now blue with orange and yellow accents.

Turret minigun is now bigger.

Skin Upgrades

The Trapper has twelve skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Five skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. One skin that can be unlocked from the Merch Shop.
  4. Four skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  5. One skin that can be obtained through Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The Plushie skin's spike traps are based on various LEGO plates.
    • The Yellow Spike Trap is based on a 1x1 LEGO Plate.
    • The Red Spike Trap is based on a 2x2 LEGO Plate.
  • The Hermit skin is a submission from the Aquatic Fantasy skin set made by 4it3dm1 and InkEve.
    • The skin was originally called "Hermit Crab" and also received a few design changes before its addition.
  • According to the Trapper Hermit Crab Charm, the Hermit skin is shy.
    • It also calls it a "Hermit Crab" when the actual skin just uses the word "Hermit". This is likely because the original skin submission used that name for the skin.
  • The Jolly skin is a submission made by OliverDalizard.

Roblox Catalog

  • The Banned skin, being a skin partially themed around older Roblox contains many references to it.
    • The hard hat worn by it is the Builders Club Hard Hat.
    • The skull worn by it is the Skull accessory while its skeleton torso comes from the Skeleton bundle.
    • The Level 4 landmine is the Subspace Tripmine gear.
    • The tools present on its belt and backpack, like the measuring tape, wrench, hammer and magic wand are all based on tools from older Roblox building games.
    • The spike traps it wears are replaced by spikes based on ones which would appear in old Roblox building games.
    • The backpack worn by the skin is based on backpacks commonly seen in older Roblox games, with one such close match being backpacks featured in Apocalypse Rising.

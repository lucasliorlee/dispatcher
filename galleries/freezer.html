Skin Upgrades

The Freezer has ten skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Two skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. One skin that can be unlocked from a Code.
  4. One skin that could have been unlocked from a Roblox code.
  5. Five skins that can be unlocked from Passes.

Skin Trivia

  • The original Mint Choco skin was made by impjelly.
  • The concept model for the Freezer was made by a community member.
  • The IcyTea skin is based on Roblox developer and YouTuber IcyTea.
  • The original IcyTea icon used its Level 3 appearance, without the gloves.
  • The Freezer used to share its upgrade icons with the Frost Blaster.
  • The Foam skin looks remarkably similar to the Breaker enemy, having a similar color scheme and stitch pattern on its torso.
  • Oddly, on Level 1 upgrade icon, the Freezer uses its Level 2 appearance, having light blue fur around its neck and a beanie.
  • The Freezer's cryo rifle may have been based on the Kriss Vector.

Roblox Catalog

  • The second, third and fourth versions of the Level 5 Default Freezer winter hood is the Subarctic Commando accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The appearance of the first version Default skin's cryogun and second version Level 0-3 Default skin's cryogun was most likely based on the Super Freeze Ray 2011 gear from the Roblox catalog.
  • The face used by the first and second version of the IcyTea skin is the Sick Day face from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Cryptid skin's hood was uploaded to the Roblox catalog as the Cryptid Hood UGC accessory.

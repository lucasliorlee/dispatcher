Skin Upgrades

The Rocketeer has fifteen skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Eleven skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. One skin that can be unlocked from Crates and Mission Quests.
  4. Two skins that can be obtained from Passes.

Skin Trivia

  • The Bosanka skin was a reference to a Bosnian soldier.
    • The name is derived from the geographical region Bosanska Krajina, a region which was heavily involved in the Bosnian War.
  • The original Bosanka skin was made by ingvaras123.
  • The version 2 of Level 0-2 Xmas skin had different reloading animation to all the other skins. Instead of kneeling down to pick up a rocket, it took out a present from its pocket and put it on the top of the present launcher.
  • The Toy skin was based on the Army men plastic soldiers.
  • The Fortress skin is a reference to the Soldier from Team Fortress 2.
    • The hat worn by the Fortress skin on Levels 3 and 4 is based on the Team Captain hat. Only on level 3 it also has a small chance of being seen with flaming particles, which is a reference to the Burning Flames Team Captain, a well-known unusual variant of the same hat.
    • The design of the rocket launcher used by the Fortress skin on Level 4 is based on the Black Box, a primary weapon for the Soldier class in Team Fortress 2.
    • The design of the radio backpack used by the Fortress skin on Level 4 is based on the Battalion's Backup, a secondary weapon for the Soldier class in Team Fortress 2.
  • The Lovestriker skin is based on Kanna from Love Bullet.
  • In multiple images and the Level 2-4 icons, the Rocketeer still use the legacy face, however, in-game, the Rocketeer uses its new face.
  • The Rocketeer currently has the second most versions for Toy skin. This record is beaten by Soldier with Doughboy skin.
  • The Tiderunner skin was made by Nebula.[1]
    • According to Nebula, the skin's name is Func.
  • The Beach skin was made by Nebula.[2]
  • The Beach skin uses a different face on the thumbnail and icon for v2.5.0.
    • This is because the skin originally had the same face as skins like Maid Commander,[3], but it ended up getting replaced by the Beach Medic's face.

Roblox Catalog

  • The Level 0-2 Pumpkin head is similar to the Sinister P. accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The Level 3-4 Pumpkin head is similar to the Sinister F accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The version 1 and 2 Level 5 head is similar to the Sinister M. accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The original Level 1-3 Default helmet is the WWII Infantry Helmet accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The original Level 4-5 Default helmet is the WWII Generals Helmet (Limited Edition) accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The version 2-3 Level 1-2 Bosanka and Default beret is a re-textured version of 1st Battalion Red Devils Beret accessory from the Roblox catalog. It is also depicts the Bosnian Army Emblem on it.
  • The version 1-4 Level 1-3 Steampunk cap is the Officer Steampunk accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The version 2 Level 2-5 Steampunk jetpack is the Steampunk Jetpack gear from the Roblox catalog.
  • The version 1 and 2 Level 0-2 Xmas skin uses the Present Launcher gear from the Roblox catalog.
  • The version 3 Level 4 skull shoulder pads used by the Pumpkin skin are the The Riddling Skull accessory from the Roblox catalog.

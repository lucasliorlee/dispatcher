Skin Upgrades

The Warden has seventeen skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Six skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. One skin that can be unlocked from a badge.
  4. Five skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  5. One skin that can be unlocked from Crates and Passes.
  6. One skin that can only be bought from the Daily Skin Store.
  7. One skin that can be unlocked upon triumphing an event map or completing a Mission Quest.
  8. One skin that can be unlocked by completing a Mission Quest.

Skin Trivia

  • The Warden seems to be based on the Night Guard from Five Nights at Freddy's 2, although he does not wield a baton.
  • The Slaughter skin is based on William Afton, the main antagonist from the Five Nights at Freddy's franchise. More accurately, it appears to be a depiction of his "purple guy" appearance.
    • The name of the skin is most likely a reference to The Man Behind the Slaughter meme.
    • The door that this skin uses as a shield is a reference to security doors in the first Five Nights at Freddy's game.
  • Interestingly, the render for the Slaughter skin which appears in the Gift Box depicts the Level 1 Slaughter Warden with the shield and axe only the Level 4 Slaughter Warden has.
  • The critical swing sound for the Default, Slaughter and Baseball skins is taken from Team Fortress 2.
    • The Ducky skin changes it to quack sounds.
  • The robot animal head the Level 4 Slaughter Warden has is Wox the Fox's endoskeleton head.
  • The bear head the Level 3 Slaughter Warden wears is based on Shadow Freddy's head from the game Five Nights at Freddy's.
  • On the render seen in the Gift Box after purchasing the Unwavering Tides Bundle gamepass and on the gamepass itself, the Pirate Warden has a different appearance Level 4 than the one in-game.
    • Most notably, the render has the Pirate Warden wear a black and blue jacket and hat, however, in-game they are actually white and blue.
    • In the render the Pirate Warden appears to have a tan skin color, while in-game it has a dark skin color.
      • This difference is not present in the trailer and thumbnail for the Unwavering Tides Update, where it has dark skin, despite both also using wrong appearances for the skins.
  • The Galactic skin appearance is based on Stormtroopers from Star Wars.
  • The Isaac skin is based on CEOofIsaac.
    • The mask at Level 4 is based on Snivy, Isaac's old mascot.
  • The Korblox skin is based on the Korblox's Empire, a Roblox medieval fantasy group.
  • The Freddy skin is based on Freddy Krueger from the Nightmare on the Elm Street franchise.
  • The Bunny skin is based on Sir Hopsalot, an enemy from the Spring 2020 Event.[1]
  • The Patriotic skin is a reference to Captain America from Marvel.

Skin Upgrades

The Commando has three skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. One skin that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. One skin that can be obtained from Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The Default skin appearance was based on the Commando from Risk of Rain 2.[1]
  • The original Level 3 and Level 4 Pirate skin shared the same clothing with the Swordmaster.
  • The Level 1 icon shows the Default skin with a missile launcher and a red headband, however, the missile launcher is not obtained until Level 3, while the red headband never appears on the skin.
  • The Trooper skin is based on the Clone commandos from Star Wars.

Roblox Catalog

  • The original Level 0-4 Default pistols were the Twin Space Pistols gear from the Roblox catalog.
    • The original Level 0-4 Pirate pistols were the Pirate's Flintlock gear from the Roblox catalog.
    • The original Level 5 Pirate pistols were the Captain's Flintlock gear from the Roblox catalog.
  • The original Level 5 Default helmet was the Exoflex Spax Helmex accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The original Level 4 Default helmet was a re-textured version of it.
  • The pirate hat on the original Level 4 Pirate was the Pirate Captain's Hat accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The eyepatch on the original Level 2-5 Pirate skin was the Eyepatch accessory from the Roblox catalog.

Update History

This only refers to cosmetic changes to the Commando. Check the main article for statistical changes.

  • 9 August 2019 - BETA v1.9.0
    • Commando added.
  • 13 October 2019 - Release v1.0.0
    • Default icon changed.
    • New skin: Pirate
  • 1 April 2020 - Release v1.8.2
    • Funny Faces added.
  • 2 April 2020 - Release v1.8.2a
    • Funny Faces removed.
  • 29 October 2020 - Overhaul v1.0.0
    • Default and Pirate Level 0-5 appearance changed.
  • 17 April 2022 - v1.2.0
    • All skins Level 0-5 appearance changed.
  • 5 August 2023 - v1.8.4
    • Pirate skin moved to the Pirate Crate.
  • 2 August 2024 - v1.27.0
    • Updated explosion visual effects.
  • 20 November 2024 - v1.42.0
    • All skins' Level 0-4 appearance changed (Level 5 removed).
    • Level 0-4 upgrade icons changed.
    • New skin: Trooper
    • Bug: Default skin has z-fighting in its torso at Level 0-3.
    • Bug: Upgrading a Level 3 Commando to Level 4 while it is attacking an enemy will make it use the Level 3 animation for the burst cooldown instead of the Level 4 animation.
    • Bug: Missile landing and explosion sounds are not affected by the Towers sound setting.

Skin Trivia

  • The image used for the Enforcer event on Roblox originally had the top and bottom path have their weapons swapped, pointing towards their weapons being swapped during development.
    • This is further supported by the flashbang models, as when upgraded to bottom path, the Enforcer begins throwing what appears to be a grenade launcher shell. While this makes little sense for bottom path, it is likely it was actually made for the current top path weapon which has a grenade launcher, though it currently fires regular flashbangs out of it.
  • The bottom path for the Abyssal skin could possibly be a reference to Big Daddy from Bioshock.

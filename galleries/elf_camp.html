Skin Trivia

  • The elves spawned by this tower are based on multiple enemies that appear in the Violent Night Event.
    • The Elf is based on the Basic Elf.
    • The Bomber Elf is based on the enemy of the same name.
    • The Guardian Elf is based on the Cookie Guard.
    • The Gift Bomber is based on the Sleigh.
    • The Ripped Elf is based on the enemy of the same name.
    • The Snowball Elf, Cannoneer Elf and Gunner Elf do not appear to be based on any enemy in the event.
  • The Elf Camp once had four additional skins alongside the Default skin, all of which are unused.
    • Oddly, the chronological sequence of the four skins and their names are the same as the Electroshocker's first four skins.
      • Additionally, the Electroshocker's icons from before the Overhaul Update of its respective skins are reused for the Elf Camp's skins. However, no model of the tower itself is displayed when equipping any of the Elf Camp's skins.
        • The Ghost skin uses the same icon as the Hazmat skin.
  • The Classic skin is based on its previous default appearance.
  • The sword used by the Guardian Elf is likely based on the Toy Sword from Reason 2 Die: Awakening.
  • The Chocolatier skin is a submission made by DaddyDonuts.

Update History

  • 21 December 2022 - v1.6.0
    • Elf Camp added.
    • Bug: Gunner Elf and Cannoneer Elf firing sounds, Ripped Elf explosion sounds and Snowball Elf hit sounds are not affected by the Towers sound setting.
  • 28 January 2024 - v1.12.0
    • Bug: Elf Camp now has Valentines, Bunny, Hazmat and Ghost skins, which do not have any models and all their information is identical to the skins of the same name for the Electroshocker.
    • Bug: The newly added skins have no icons.
  • 10 May 2024 - v1.21.0
    • Bug: Guardian Elf and Snowball Elf walk animations are not affected by Time Scale.
  • 2 August 2024 - v1.27.0
    • Updated explosion visual effects for all units except the Ripped Elf.
  • 9 April 2025 - v1.60.0
    • Bugfix: Valentines, Bunny, Hazmat and Ghost now display their icons.
  • 16 April 2025 - v1.61.0
    • Bug: Ripped Elf no longer has animations.
  • 14 May 2025 - v1.64.0
    • Bugfix: Ripped Elf now has animations again.
  • 18 October 2025 - v1.77.0
    • Bugfix: Gunner Elf and Cannoneer Elf firing sounds, Ripped Elf explosion sounds and Snowball Elf hit sounds are now affected by the Towers sound setting.
  • 20 December 2025 - v1.83.0
    • Default Level 0-4 appearance changed.
    • New skin: Classic
    • Bug: Default Gunner Elf has grey cubes in its guns.
    • Bug: Default Gunner Elf no longer fires any projectiles, though it still deals damage.
    • Bug: Certain parts of the default skin have broken textures.
    • Bug: Default skin has an outdated tower pet.
    • Bugfix: Elf Camp no longer has Valentines, Bunny, Hazmat and Ghost skins.
  • 11 February 2026 - v1.85.0
    • New skin: Chocolatier
    • Bug: Chocolatier Guardian Elf wields two swords at once.

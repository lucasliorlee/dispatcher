Skin Upgrades

The Toxic Gunner has two skins in total, those being:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. One skin that can be unlocked from Crates.

Skin Trivia

  • Prior to The Final Act Event, Toxic Gunner's icon upgrades were shared with the Soldier.
  • There is a Level 4 Toxic Gunner in the Solar Eclipse Event trailer that appears to be wielding the gun the Level 0-2 Toxic Gunner wields.

Roblox Catalog

  • The mask used by the original Level 2 and 3 Toxic Gunner is a re-textured version of the Firefighters Breather Amber accessory from the Roblox catalog.

Update History

This only refers to cosmetic changes to the Toxic Gunner. Check the main article for statistical changes.

  • 8 May 2021 - Overhaul v1.3.0
    • Toxic Gunner added.
  • 17 April 2022 - v1.2.0
    • Default Level 0-4 appearance changed.
  • 21 December 2023 - v1.11.0
    • New skin: Phantom
    • Bug: Phantom Level 4 muzzle flash is slightly off center.
  • 21 August 2024 - v1.28.0
    • Bug: Toxic Gunner animations break after the first shot.
  • 16 October 2024 - v1.36.0
    • Bugfix: Toxic Gunner animations no longer break after the first shot.
  • 20 December 2025 - v1.83.0
    • All skins Level 0-4 appearance changed.
    • Bug: Neons on the vest of the Level 2 default skin have z-fighting.
    • Bug: Phantom skin has an outdated icon.
  • 21 August 2026 - v2.7.0
    • Bugfix: Phantom skin icon is now up to date.

Skin Trivia

  • The Remington and Dragunov rifles that the original Hunter gained at Level 2 and Level 5 respectively on all skins may be a reference to the Reason 2 Die: Awakening versions of these weapons, a game which BelowNatural has previously worked on.
    • Additionally, the M1 Garand that the original Hunter initially carried on all skins was inspired by the original R2D version of this weapon.
  • In real life, the M21 is gas-operated, meaning you can fire it constantly without bolting. But in-game, the Level 3 Hunter inaccurately bolts it anyways.
  • Even though the M1 Garand is a semi-automatic rifle in real life, the original Hunter would pull back a bolt manually, which is historically inaccurate as the M1 Garand does not have one.
    • The M1 Garand is also an infantry rifle, meaning it is not made for hunting.
  • The original Level 5 Default knife that is sheathed was the Restricted Access Rambo Knife that BelowNatural made for R2DA.
    • The original Level 5 Halloween knife was the Halloween Rambo Knife from R2DA.
  • The chainsaw on the back of the original Level 5 Halloween skin is the Chainsaw used in R2D and R2DA.
  • For the original Halloween skin, the Falcon was replaced by the Bat, the same one that appears in Nightmare Carnival.
  • Despite being released at Halloween, along with other Halloween 2019 Crate skins, the Halloween skin is not considered to be a Halloween skin, but rather a coded skin.
    • The Halloween skin was the first skin to be released exclusively as a code.
  • The original Blue skin looked similar to the original design of the Hunter when it was planned to be called the Clout God.
    • This accessory was added way after the Hunter was added.
  • The Level 3+ Vampire Slayer skin will look through its scope with its right eye despite having an eyepatch.
  • The Scuba Ops skin was made by Linx.[1]

Roblox Catalog

  • The Level 0-1 Halloween skin cap is the Biker Cap accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The original Level 4-5 shades used by the Hunter is the Midnight-Shades Midnight Shades accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The cap used by the original Hunter was a re-textured version of the Green Military Winter Cap, but with a twitter logo instead of a paradoxum logo.

Skin Upgrades

The Engineer has seventeen skins in total. These include:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Four skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. Five skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. Three skins that can be unlocked from both Crates and Passes.
  5. One skin that can be unlocked from the Merch Shop.
  6. Two skins that can only be purchased through the Daily Store.
  7. One skin that can be obtained through Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The Engineer's Level 4 upgrade icon is the Minigun Icon from Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas.
  • There is a small chance that one of the Engineer's War Machine Sentries will spawn with a teddy bear on it.
    • This is a reference to the now removed Sentry tower, which had a 30% chance of having a teddy bear on it prior to the MEGA Update.
  • The Level 2 upgrade icon is the same as the Turret's Level 1 and 2 icon.
  • The Mechanic skin is a reference to the RED Engineer from Team Fortress 2.
  • The radio the Mechanic, Holiday and Heartbreak skins wear resembles the Sapper from Team Fortress 2.
  • The Wikia skin is based on WikiaColors.
    • Coincidentally, the skin was added on their birthday, the 28th of January.
  • The CrazRex skin is based on CrazRex.
    • When the skin was first added, it was misspelled as DraxRex.
    • This skin was made as a reward for being one of the first people to triumph Infernal Abyss on Hardcore mode, alongside DanksPlatter and 5ouls. All of them received a skin.

Roblox Catalog

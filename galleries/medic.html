Skin Upgrades

The Medic has fifteen skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Nine skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. Two skins that can be obtained from Passes.
  4. Two skins that can be obtained through Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The syringe gun that the original Default and Witch skins initially have is similar to the Syringe Gun from Team Fortress 2.
  • On the original Level 2–4 Default skin, Vitamins from R2DA can be seen on the belt.
  • The original Level 5 Cyber skin may have been loosely based on Mercy from Overwatch.
  • Oddly, prior to Medic's v1.73.0 rework, the Level 5 Stranded skin could fire the light beams that other skins use at that level, despite normally firing bullets with its pistol.
    • Some details like the face have already been added to the game, though other differences still exist.
  • The Shamrock skin is based on Vocaloid Megpoid, more commonly known as GUMI.
    • This skin contains multiple differences, most prominently the lack of goggles, compared to various official renders. However, it may be added back in the future.[1]

    • The hair used by the Level 0–4 Bunny skin is a re-textured version of the Platinum Short Style accessory from the Roblox catalog.
      • The hair used by the Level 5 Bunny skin uses a retextured version of the Black Bun with Waves accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The hood used by the Level 1–4 Bunny skin uses a recolored version of the Fluffy Pink Bunny Hood accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The first and second versions of the Valentines skin used the Wink-Blink face from the Roblox catalog.
  • The first, second, and third version of the Level 5 Default skin uses the Erisyphia Staff gear from the Roblox catalog.
    • The Level 1–4 Default mask is the Surgeon's Mask accessory from the Roblox catalog.

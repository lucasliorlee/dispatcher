Skin Trivia

  • Despite having a humanoid, none of the skins had a Funny Face since they are all faceless.
  • The same humanoid used in the previous Ace Pilot variants is also used in the Military Base and Mecha Base.
  • The original Level 3 icon shows a blue monoplane, which did not appear in-game with any skin at the time.
  • Technically, as the Grumman F6F Hellcat and F22 Raptor (and originally the A6M5 Zero and the F-35 Lightning II fighter jet) are at a cruising altitude, and both models have retractable landing gears, they should not have their landing gears deployed.
    • The Hotshot skin has aircraft with retractable landing gear. But they appear to be up.
  • The bomb bay on the Level 2 and higher Ace Pilot will open when its dropping a bomb.
  • The Level 1-2 aircraft for the unreleased Hotshot skin is a Piper Cub, armed with machine guns on its wings.
    • The Level 3-4 aircraft resembles a North American P-51 Mustang fighter aircraft with the iconic invasion stripes livery, also known as the D-Day markings.
    • The Level 5 aircraft is the iconic Fairchild Republic A-10 Thunderbolt II "Warthog" with the iconic "Shark mouth" paint.
      • Ironically, despite having the powerful and famous GAU-8 Minigun in front of the aircraft, three machine gun pods can be seen attached to the undersides of both wings. This could mean that the GAU-8 at front of the A-10 does not do anything, and the only guns that fire are the gun pods.
  • The Hotshot skin appears in Wrecked Battlefield II, despite not being present in-game.
    • However, they are not the same as the ones posted in the Tower Defense Simulator Discord. Most notably, the stripes on wings and the spades are removed.

Update History

This only refers to cosmetic changes to the Ace Pilot. Check the main article for statistical changes.

  • 29 November 2019 - Release v1.4.0
    • Ace Pilot added.
    • Skins: Red, Green, Yellow
  • 29 October 2020 - Overhaul v1.0.0
    • All skins Level 0-5 appearance changed.
    • New skins: Aerial Ace, Navy, Purple
  • 31 October 2020 - Overhaul v1.1.0
    • New skin: Pumpkin
  • 29 December 2021
    • Bug: All of the Ace Pilot's skins do not upgrade properly.
  • 29 December 2021 - v1.0.0l
    • Bugfix: All of the Ace Pilot's skins now upgrade properly.
  • 21 June 2024 - v1.25.0
    • All skins appearance changed.
    • Updated explosion visual effects.
    • Bug: When upgraded to Level 3 and 5, duplicate parts from the Level 0-2 plane will fall from the runway into the void.
    • Bug: At Level 5, the minigun for the default plane model used at that Level is actually off center and does not even match the hole made for it. Applies to any future skins which also use the exact same plane.
  • 2 August 2024 - v1.27.0
    • Updated explosion visual effects.
  • 18 September 2024 - v1.32.0
    • Bug: Level 0-2 Ace Pilot appears in the Level 3+ preview of the upgrade menu, alongside the later planes.
  • 16 April 2025 - v1.61.0
    • New skin: Easter
  • 20 December 2025 - v1.83.0a
    • New skin: Toy Plane
    • Bug: Toy Plane is upside down in all of its previews, such as the upgrade preview or the inventory.

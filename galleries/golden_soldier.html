  1. The Default skin.

  3. Five skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. Four skins that can be unlocked from a Crate and a Pass.
  5. One skin that can be unlocked upon triumphing an event map.

Skin Trivia

  • The Cold Soldier skin was originally based on the Winter Soldier in the Marvel Universe.
    • The Cold Soldier was originally planned to be named Bucky Barnes or Winter Soldier.
    • Hilariously, the Cold Soldier is referred as the "Cold Soldier Soldier" in game.
  • The original Cold Soldier icon used its Level 5 upgrade, without the shades and with an M16A2 instead of a G36.
  • The first version of the Default skin's Level 4+ armor was the Flakvest from R2DA.
    • The same mesh was used for the Level 4 armor for the second version of the Default skin, and the first version of the Red, Blue and Party skins, though in a different color.
  • The original version of the Toy skin's Level 3-4 gun is the NERF-Fortnite-AR-L-Elite-Blaster.
  • The Toy and Golden Soldier have unique shooting sounds.
  • The Toy skin is based on the Army men plastic soldiers.
  • The Doughboy skin is a reference to the American Infantrymen during World War One, who were popularly called doughboys.
  • There were multiple times where the Doughboy skin's animations and upgrades were bugged and later fixed.
  • The Holiday Soldier skin's backpack disappears at Level 3 and reappears at Level 4.
    • This may have been a developer mistake.
  • The Liberator skin is based on the Helldivers uniform.
  • The Grand Theft skin's name appears to be a reference to the Grand Theft Auto series, though the skin itself does not appear to resemble anything from any game until Level 4.
    • The Level 4 appearance has a horse head which resembles the Chestnut Horse mask purchasable in Grand Theft Auto Online.
  • The Stealth Ops skin is based on Helghast Soldiers from the Killzone franchise.
    • The skin was originally called Extrasolar Warrior[1] before being changed to its current name. The former name references the Extrasolar Wars.
  • The Dark Frost skin on its in-game icon skin has purple skin, however, this is mostly not the case in-game.
    • Its torso and legs are still purple, though this is only clearly visible at Level 4.
    • This was likely a mistake caused by the Dark Frost skin being confused with the Frost Legion skins, which actually do use purple skin tones.
  • The Beach skin was made by supereroulnoptizet.[2]
    • The Beach skin was originally made as a Frost Blaster skin, however, it ended up getting converted into a Soldier skin instead.[3][4]
      • Certain parts of the design like the Frost Blaster weapon look did get carried over to the Soldier version of the skin.[5]

Roblox Catalog

  • The Default, Red, Green, Blue, Bunny, Cold Soldier, Golden and Toy skins' cap is a re-textured version of the French Brimmed Cap accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 4 beret used by multiple Soldier skins is the Shotgunner's Beret accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • This item can actually be gotten from the Roblox Mystery Figure - Shotgunner Roblox toy.
  • The Level 4 goggles used by the Soldier is a re-textured version of the Laser Spec Special Forces Goggles accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 2-3 shades used by the Cold Soldier is the Midnight Shades accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Cold Soldier skin's Level 4 ushanka is the Very Black Ushanka from the Roblox catalog.
  • The first version of the Golden skin's Level 2-4 paintball mask is the Golden Paintball Mask accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The first version of the Cold Soldier skin used the Serious Scar Face from the Roblox catalog.
  • The first version of the default skin's Level 2+ radio is the Airstrike gear from the Roblox catalog.
    • The first version of the default skin's Level 5 helmet is the Operation Desert Cyclone accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The first version of the default skin's Level 5 hood, bandanna and goggles are the Desert Scout accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Beast Slayer skin's hat was uploaded to the Roblox catalog as the Monster's Skull UGC accessory.

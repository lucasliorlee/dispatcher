Skin Upgrades

The Sniper has twelve skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Seven skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. Three skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. One skin that can be unlocked from both Crates and Passes.

Skin Trivia

  • Prior to the 4th of March 2020, the Sniper originally had a KAC M110 SASS rifle and laid in a prone position.
    • Prior to the Overhaul Update, the Sniper had a position similar to that of the Outlaw.
  • The original Ghillie skin was made by ice22343.
  • The Silent skin appears to be a reference to Simo Häyhä, also known as The White Death.
    • He was a Finnish sniper who participated in the Winter War. It was believed he had killed over 500 enemy soldiers during the war and was believed to be the deadliest sniper in history.
  • The Sniper, despite wielding a Barrett M82 at Level 4, which is a semi-automatic weapon, still chambers a round.
    • The previous version of the Sniper did not do this.

Roblox Catalog

  • The original Level 3-4 helmet and mask used by most Sniper skins is the Sniper Relay Helmet accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The original Level 2 goggles on most skins are a re-textured version of the Laser Spec Special Forces Goggles accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The second version of the Level 2-3 Ghillie skin used the Spy Vision 7500 accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The second version of the Level 4-5 Ghillie skin used the Ghillie Hat accessory from the Roblox catalog.

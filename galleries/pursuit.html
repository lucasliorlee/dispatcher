Skin Upgrades

The Pursuit has five skins. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. One skin that can be obtained from Crates.
  3. Two skins that can be obtained from Passes.
  4. One skin that can be unlocked from a Code.
  5. One skin that can be unlocked from Skincrates.

Skin Trivia

  • The Pursuit models were made by JohnDrinkin.
  • The PMC skin's Level 1-3 model is based on the Bell H-13 Sioux, armed with a weapons pod on each of its wings.
    • The Level 4 model for the PMC skin is the famous Sikorsky UH-61 Blackhawk helicopter, with extra wings attached along with rocket launchers being on the underside of each.
    • The Level 5 model for the PMC skin is the Russian MI-24 Hind transport helicopter. Appearing in normal weapon configuration.
  • Before the Overhaul Update, two Soldiers could be seen inside the Pursuit on the Level 4 upgrade icon, even though no person could be seen inside the Pursuit at the time.
  • The second Level 2 and Level 3 upgrade icons depict two people controlling the Pursuit, but only one person is seen controlling the helicopter at these levels.
  • The in-game icon for the Default Pursuit shows the pilots of the helicopter not having any animations.
  • The Eggy skin was renamed to Easter after v1.71.0.

Update History

This only refers to cosmetic changes to the Pursuit. Check the main article for statistical changes.

  • 26 May 2020 - Release v1.11.0
    • Pursuit added.
  • 29 October 2020 - Overhaul v1.0.0
    • Default Level 0-5 appearance changed.
  • 2 August 2024 - v1.27.0
    • Updated explosion visual effects.
  • 27 November 2024 - v1.43.0
    • Default Level 0-5 appearance changed.
      • Added bottom path Level 4-5, previous Level 4-5 are now the top path.
    • Level 0-5 upgrade icons changed.
    • Updated placement sound.
    • Bug: Has broken animations in the inventory preview.
    • Bug: Default skin has an incorrect icon.
    • Bug: Miniguns do not rev-up properly.
  • 16 April 2025 - v1.61.0
    • New skin: Eggy
    • Bug: Eggy skin has a small grey cube inside of it.
  • 11 June 2025 - v1.67.0
    • Bugfix: Eggy skin no longer has a small grey cube inside of it, except in the lobby.
  • 23 July 2025 - v1.71.0
    • Easter skin renamed from Eggy to Easter.
    • Reduced polygon count of the Easter and default skins.
    • Bug: Easter skin uses the default pilot model instead of the duck pilot model.
    • Bug: Easter skin is still called Eggy in the inventory on mobile.
    • Bugfix: Easter no longer has a visible grey cube in the lobby.
  • 18 October 2025 - v1.77.0
    • New skin: Dragon
    • Bug: Dragon skin does not have a tower pet.
  • 5 June 2026 - v2.0.0
    • Bugfix: Miniguns now rev-up correctly.
  • 8 June 2026 - v2.0.6a
    • New skin: Mercenary
    • Bug: Mercenary skin has windows made out of opaque grey plastic and there is no pilots in the helicopter.
  • 9 June 2026 - v2.0.7a
    • Bugfix: Mercenary skin windows are now translucent and the helicopter now has pilots.
  • 2 July 2026 - v2.1.7
    • New skin: Patriotic
    • Bug: Some Patriotic skins sounds do not play.
  • 5 July 2026 - v2.2.1a
    • Bugfix: All Patriotic skin sounds now play.

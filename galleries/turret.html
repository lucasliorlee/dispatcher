Skin Upgrades

The Turret has seven skins in total. These include:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Two skins that can be obtained from Crates.
  3. Three skins that can be obtained from Passes.
  4. One skin that can be obtained through Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The first version of XR300 skin was made by LilDisquit.
  • Although the Level 4 upgrade icon shows an explosive warhead, the Turret cannot deal explosive damage at any level.
  • The XR500 skin is a reference to the Reason 2 Die's XR500 Xbox mount.
    • It is operated by an Outpost 32 guard.
    • It was also the only Turret skin to have a Funny Face.
  • The Turret was the oldest tower to have not received a single appearance change to any of its skins in the game since it was added, a period of over 6 years from the 5th of August 2019 to the 20th of August 2025.
  • The Crossbow skin may be inspired by the X-Bow from Clash of Clans.
  • The operator of the Bunny skin has the non-binary flag on one of its sleeves starting from Level 2, which according to the operator's creator is due to the operator being non-binary.[1]
  • The default appearance of the Turret at Level 0-2 seems to resemble the removed Sentry tower.

This only refers to cosmetic changes to the Turret. Check the main article for statistical changes.

    • Turret added.

    • New skin: XR500

    • New skin: XR300

    • Funny Faces added.
  • 2 April 2020 - Release v1.8.2a
    • Funny Faces removed.
  • 28 October 2023 - v1.9.0g
    • New skin: Crossbow
    • Bug: XR500 has broken animations and sound.
  • 14 May 2024 - v1.21.6
    • Bug: Crossbow no longer faces enemies correctly at Level 3 and higher.
    • Bugfix: XR500 animations and sound fixed.
  • 20 November 2024 - v1.42.0
    • Bugfix: Crossbow now faces enemies correctly at Level 3 and higher.
  • 20 August 2025 - v1.73.0
    • All skins' Level 0-5 appearance changed.
    • New skin: Jetski
    • Bug: Bullet trails still appear when Bullet Trails setting is disabled.
    • Bug: Crossbow muzzle flash points upside down when shooting at Level 0-2 and Level 5.
    • Bug: Turret does not appear correctly on the triumph screen.
    • Bug: When shielding the Jetski skin, the Medic's shield appears above the tower instead of on it.
    • Bug: Jetski skin is positioned very low in the tower preview. All other skins are instead placed higher.
    • Bug: XR300 skin has a single miscolored neon at Level 5.
  • 18 October 2025 - v1.77.0
    • Bugfix: Bullet trails no longer appear when Bullet Trails setting is disabled.
  • 20 December 2025 - v1.83.0a
    • New skin: Grinch
    • Bug: Grinch skin has a large clickbox.
  • 3 April 2026 - v1.88.0
    • New skin: Bunny
    • Bug: Bunny skin has no icon in the loadout.
    • Bug: Bunny skin has huge hitbox.
  • 8 May 2026 - v1.90.0

Skin Upgrades

The Executioner has four skins. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. One skin that can only be unlocked from the Daily Skin Store.
  3. One skin that was obtained by owning the Executioner before the 24th of November 2023.
  4. One skin that can be obtained from Crates and Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • The default skin is modeled by Nebula.[1]
    • The default skin's concept art was drawn by Tylerdagoat.[2]
  • The default skin has a cowl on Level 2 onwards, this is based on the unused initial design for the Executioner, which was present on various teasers but did not end up getting added into the game.[3]
  • The Level 5 appearance of the default skin is based on The Stalker.[4]
  • The current default skin's armor was modeled after the Eclipse Mortar's armor.[5]
  • The default skin's bun is shaped like an axe.
    • This was supposed to remain on all levels, however was scrapped.[6]
  • The Level 5 Eclipse skin armor may be inspired by The Umbra's armor.
    • The Executioner enemy is also based on this skin.
  • The Default skin originally was meant to wear a cowl on every Level, though this did not make it into the game.
    • This appearance can still be seen on a few images officially released by the developers, though it appears to be the Level 5 appearance based on the axe it is wielding in them.
  • The Vanquisher skin is based on the Inquisitors, most notably the Fifth Brother from Star Wars.

Roblox Catalog

  • The axe used on the Level 2 icon is the Zombie STOPing Axe from the Roblox catalog.

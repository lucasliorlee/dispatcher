Skin Trivia

  • The default skin may be a reference to Guts from Berserk.
  • The Rockstar skin is a submission modeled by SoupedGun.
    • The concept art for the skin was made by Joe0w44.
    • The skin was heavily inspired by Susie from the game Deltarune, a mix of the fashion of Punk and with the general idea of rockstars in the 70s.
    • Its weapon took a lot of inspiration from Marceline's Axe Guitar from the show Adventure Time, as well as The Axe from the game Terraria.
    • "Warlocks Rule" is a reference to "Wizards Rule", a phrase in Adventure Time that unlocks the entrance to the Wizard City.
    • The Rockstar skin had to have its arm amputated in the past, resulting in its unusual appearance.
      • The part of the amputated arm that still remains has less muscles on it. This was done to make it match up with what real amputees typically have.
  • The Surfs Up skin was made by null while the concept art was made by Loonimoff.[1]
  • The Dragon skin was made by Linx.[2]
  • The Dragon skin is missing a wing and is blind in one eye, apparently caused by some injury.[3]

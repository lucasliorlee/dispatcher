Skin Upgrades

The Paintballer has four skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Three skins that can be unlocked from Crates.

Skin Trivia

  • The first version of the Default skin was originally based on YouTuber JOHN ROBLOX.
    • On 16th of June 2019, the Paintballer was re-modelled to resemble a paintballer instead of JOHN ROBLOX.
  • Even though the Green skin is only a recolor like skins from the Basic Crate, it is a part of the Premium Crate.
    • However, unlike the other skins, it changes the colors of the Paintballer's projectile and explosions.

Roblox Catalog

  • The original paintball gun wielded by the second version of the Default Paintballer and first version of the Red Paintballer between Level 0-1 was the Paintball Gun from Roblox BrickBattles.
    • The original paintball gun wielded between Level 2-5 was the Paintball Pro gear in the Roblox catalog.
      • Currently, all skins between Levels 0-2 use the same mesh for their paintball gun, but with a different texture.
  • The second version of the Default Level 5 paintball mask is the Neon Paintball Mask accessory from the Roblox catalog.

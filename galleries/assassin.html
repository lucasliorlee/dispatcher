Skin Upgrades

The Assassin has six skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Two skins that can be obtained from Crates.
  3. Two skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. One skin that can be obtained through Mission Quests.

Skin Trivia

  • Weirdly, the Assassin icon uses the Slasher's face instead of its own face.
  • The Pumpkin skin was modelled by ElectroMeltdown while its concept art was made by Nyazh with the help of Tylerrington.[1]
  • The papers on the back of the Pumpkin skin are a reference to Slenderman, with the one talking about a mad king also referencing Lord Sinister.[2]
    • The skin was originally going to have more creepypasta references if that would be allowed.[2]

Update History

This only refers to cosmetic changes to the Assassin. Check the main article for statistical changes.

  • 6 August 2025 - v1.72.0
    • Assassin added.
    • Bug: Level 4 has a small grey cube inside it.
    • Bug: Tower pet has no animations.
    • Bug: Assassin does not stop moving when frozen by both legacy and modern freeze from enemies.
    • Bug: Assassin's frozen body does not have animations.
    • Bug: Level 2's goggle lenses are wrongly positioned inside the Assassin's head.
  • 20 December 2025 - v1.83.0
    • New skin: Actor
  • 20 December 2025 - v1.83.0a
    • New skin: Saber Tooth Tiger
      • Bug: Saber Tooth Tiger has no upgrade models, and uses the default machete when at Level 4.
  • 3 April 2026 - v1.88.0
    • New skin: SciBunny
  • 22 May 2026 - v1.91.0
    • New skin: Crew
  • 18 September 2026 - v2.11.0
    • New skin: Pumpkin
    • Bug: Pumpkin throwing knives are made out of grey plastic.
    • Bug: Pumpkin uses the Level 4 upgrade changes and throwing knives on all Levels.
  • 18 September 2026 - v2.11.1
    • Bugfix: Pumpkin now has proper upgrade models.

Skin Upgrades

The Shotgunner has sixteen skins in total. This includes:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. One skin that is currently unobtainable.
  3. Six skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  4. Four skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  5. Two skin that can be unlocked from both Crates and Passes.
  6. One skin that can be unlocked from both Mission Quests and Crates.
  7. One skin that can be unlocked from a Code.

Skin Trivia

  • Originally, the Shotgunner when it was called Enforcer was meant to have a SWAT shield at Level 5. This, however, was not added.
  • The original Level 3-5 Hallow Punk skin used the Pumpkin Vest from R2DA.
  • The current Slayer skin is based on Doom Guy from DOOM.
    • The original Slayer skin was heavily based on Doom Slayer from DOOM .
    • The Level 5 double barrel shotgun used by the Slayer skin during Hardcore Testing BETA was likely based on the Super Shotgun from DOOM.
  • The Classic skin is based on the original design of the Default Shotgunner.
  • The Mega Slow and Slow Boss could be seen behind Shotgunner on the second Level 5 upgrade icon.
  • The Trumpeteer skin was a submission conceptualized and modeled by SoupedGun.
    • The skin was inspired by jazz artists from the 1940s and 1950s, specifically from the New Orleans area.
    • Originally, the skin was going to have an Elvis-styled pompadour and custom animations, where it would sit on a stool and blow the trumpet to deal damage.
  • The Scuba Ops skin was made by Linx.[1]

Roblox Catalog

  • The original Level 0-3 Default hat was a re-textured version of the Police Sergeants Cap accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The original Level 2 Default badge was the Salute gear from the Roblox catalog.
  • Originally, there was a glitch where the Hallow Punk would have a black torso between Level 0-3, instead of an orange torso.
    • The shield that would be used was the Riot Shield and Billy Club Set gear from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 2-4 Hallow Punk wears the Badass Shades for Badass Individuals accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The Level 5 Hallow Punk wears the Red Badass Shades for Badass Individuals accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 4-5 Hallow Punk cap is the Biker Cap accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 2-4 Hallow Punk headphones are the Extreme Headphones accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The second versions of the Level 2-4 Default, Classic and Spooky skins wore the Midnight Shades accessory from the Roblox catalog.
    • The Level 5 shades were a re-textured version of it.
  • The original Level 4-5 Classic skin used a re-textured version of the Daring Blonde Beard accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • The Level 4-5 Slayer skin used a re-textured version of the The Bug Stomper accessory from the Roblox catalog.

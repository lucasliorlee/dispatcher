Skin Upgrades

The Demoman has fifteen skins in total. These include:

  1. The Default skin.
  2. Ten skins that can be unlocked from Crates.
  3. Three skins that can be unlocked from Passes.
  4. One skin that can be unlocked from triumphing a limited-time map.

Skin Trivia

  • The first and second versions of the Level 3, 4 and 5 Default skin were based on Team Fortress 2's RED Demoman as they wore a black vest with a red shirt underneath.
    • The original weapon used by the Demoman is based on the stickybomb launcher, also from Team Fortress 2.
    • The Fortress skin is a more direct reference to the RED Demoman.
  • The upgrade icons show the Demoman with a red torso. However, it has a grey torso.
    • These upgrade icons were made during Hardcore Testing BETA, when the Demoman still had a red torso.
  • Oddly, the Demoman has no Level 1 icon.
    • This was fixed in the Violent Night Update, however, on the 21st of March 2023, the upgrade icon would be removed.
  • The Military skin was in the game before the Violent Night Update, however, due to a bug it was unobtainable.
  • The Egg skin shares the same yellow egg head design as the Zombegg enemy.
  • The Beach skin was made by mitzu.[1]

Roblox Catalog

  • The first and second versions weapon used in game is the Newtrat's Tuskinator gear from the Roblox catalog.

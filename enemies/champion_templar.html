Ability 1
Cooldown: 40
Attack CD: 5

Weaponry Switch - The Champion Templar takes 2.5 seconds to alternate between its two weapons: the minigun and the hammer.
While using its minigun, The Hammer Throw and Fiery Boon abilities are deactivated. Whereas when using its hammer, the Focused Fire ability is deactivated.

Ability 2
Cooldown: 6
Attack CD: 6

Focused Fire - The Champion Templar locks onto a target and charges up a powerful shot from its minigun. After a short delay, it unleashes a continuous bullet barrage for 4 seconds, dealing 600 damage to units in a wide, growing beam and stunning towers caught in the path for 4 seconds. Has a firerate of 0.05 and a range of 20.

Ability 3
Cooldown: 10
Attack CD: 6

Hammer Throw - The Champion Templar hurls a massive hammer with great force, creating a shockwave upon impact. The explosion deals 600 damage to all units in a large radius, stuns towers for 4 seconds, and applies a 25% Range Debuff for the same duration as the stun. Has an explosion radius of 10 while the attack itself has a range of 25.

Ability 4
Cooldown: 20
Attack CD: 6

Fiery Boon - The Champion Templar slams the ground, creating a defensive aura that boosts nearby enemies' defense by 100% for 30 seconds through the Blessed attribute. The impact also deals 600 damage to nearby units and stuns towers for 4 seconds. Has a range of 15.

Description

The Champion Templar is the final boss of Challenge Trials and all three PVP arenas. It has higher health than all enemies that appear before it and has multiple abilities capable of stunning towers, damaging units and supporting enemies.

It has four abilities, with one of them being Weaponry Switch. This makes it swap between its hammer and minigun for 2.5 seconds. Depending on which mode is currently active, it can use different abilities.

When wielding its minigun, it can only use its Focused Fire ability. This makes it lock onto a target within its range of 20 units, then charge up a powerful shot. After a short delay will cause it to fire with a firerate of 0.05, dealing 600 damage to units and stunning towers for 4 seconds. It will keep firing for 4 seconds.

When wielding its hammer, it has two abilities, Hammer Throw and Fiery Boon.

  1. Hammer Throw makes the Champion Templar throw its hammer at a target within its range of 25 units, creating an explosion with a radius of 10 on impact. This explosion applies a 4 second stun and 4 second 25% Range Debuff to towers, while also dealing 600 damage to units.
  2. Fiery Boon makes the Champion Templar slam the ground with its hammer, creating a defense aura that makes enemies, including itself, Blessed. The impact from the aura will also damage all units by 600 and stun towers for 4 seconds within its range of 15.

Appearance

Red TeamBlue Team

The Champion Templar wears gold and white armor with red accents, two bulky shoulder pads each with a spike on top, a knight-like helmet with two eye sockets and silver horns. Various parts of its armor contain gold crosses. It wields a hammer-minigun fusion weapon which can switch between a minigun and hammer mode.

The Champion Templar wears white and black armor with silver accents, two bulky shoulder pads each with a spike on top, a knight-like helmet with two eye sockets and silver horns. Various parts of its armor contain black crosses. It wields a hammer-minigun fusion weapon which can switch between a minigun and hammer mode.

Strategy

  • The Champion Templar is backed by plenty of Brutes, Molten Titans and Fallen Guardians. It is advised to bring high-end towers like the Accelerator, Necromancer, Mercenary Base and Minigunner to defeat it.
    • Unstunnable towers like Pursuit and Ranger can be useful against the Champion Templar as they can consistently deal full damage without being stunned.
  • The Champion Templar boasts extremely short cooldowns with heavy stuns, so towers like the Medic can be helpful to counteract Focused Fire and Hammer Throw.
  • High-end DPS towers are suggested to take down Blessed enemies, and defense melting towers like the Pyromancer are invaluable to have, due to the incredibly high defense values.
    • Although it may seem like due to Blessed, defense melting towers would be far more effective, this is not actually the case, as the value melted does not change. For instance, with 100% Defense, the Pyromancer will reduce it to 50%. If that same enemy has Blessed, the Pyromancer will not reduce it to 100% but instead will still reduce it down by 50% to 150%.
    • Mortar and Rocketeer can be great picks as well, since they completely negate defense, and can chew down the horde to reduce Fiery Boon effectiveness.
  • Baiting out the Champion Templar's attacks can be incredibly useful, as its attacks ravage your defending capabilities otherwise.

Trivia

  • The Champion Templar Logbook can only be obtained through Challenge Trials.
  • The Champion Templar is the first and currently the only PVP enemy to have different appearances based on the team it spawned in.
    • If spawned outside of PVP mode (most notably in Challenge Trials), it would take the appearance of the Red Team spawn.
  • The Champion Templar is a reimagining of the legacy Templar enemy.
    • Because of this, it is likely named after the Knights Templar, a Catholic medieval chivalric order, most infamously known for the Crusades.
  • The Champion Templar is unaffected by enemy appearance modifiers, such as Talk Like a Pirate Day.
  • Interestingly, inside the Champion Templar’s torso lies a mini Champion Templar with no animations.

Description

The Hidden Boss is a large and fast boss that appears in Casual, Legacy Molten, Intermediate, Molten, Base Arena PVP and Molten Arena PVP modes. It also appeared in The Classic Event. It has decent amount of health and decent speed. Towers must have hidden detection to target the Hidden Boss.

The Hidden Boss can be spawned from the Mystery Boss upon its death in the Easy and Legacy Molten modes.

Appearance

ModernLegacy

CurrentVersion 1

The current version of the Hidden Boss is a large humanoid with a waist, lower arms and lower legs in its rig. It is faceless and has a translucent dark grey body with a slime-like texture. It wears a black blindfold and cloak with a cape and sleeves, as well as black spiked braces with chains attached. It emits black smoke from its body.

The first version of the Hidden Boss was a large humanoid with a waist in its rig. It had a translucent black body and wears a black cape, cloak and sleeves along with a grey blindfold.

It wears a black cape and a large pair of translucent black horns with a shorter pair behind them. It has an emissive white scar on its face with two streaks leaking on the right side of it.

The current Enemies Beta version of the legacy Hidden Boss is a large humanoid with a waist in its rig. It has a translucent black body and wears a black cape, cloak and sleeves along with a grey blindfold.

The third version of the legacy Hidden Boss had translucent black skin and a black pebble torso and legs. It wore a black cape and had a large pair of translucent black horns with a shorter pair behind them. It had a drooling zombie face with glowing white eyes.

The second version of the legacy Hidden Boss had translucent grey skin and a dark navy granite torso and legs. It wore a black cape and had a large pair of translucent black horns with a shorter pair behind them. It had a drooling zombie face with glowing white eyes. Grey smoke also came out of its torso.

The first version of the legacy Hidden Boss had translucent grey skin and a translucent dark grey torso and legs. It had a large pair of translucent black horns with a shorter pair behind them. It had a drooling zombie face with glowing white eyes. White particles also came out of its torso. Its walking animation had it hovering above the path.

Strategy

  • Towers with decent DPS and hidden detection, like the Minigunner, Hunter and Crook Boss can be used to deal with it.
    • Early-game towers like the Cowboy, Soldier, Militant or Assassin can also be used.
  • Towers like the Ace Pilot or Firework Technician can be used to grant hidden detection to towers that do not normally have it, like the Ranger, Mortar or Toxic Gunner.
  • While some splash damage towers like the Demoman and Mortar cannot target Hidden Bosses, they can hit them through splash damage, if another detectable enemy is near the Hidden Boss.
    • This is very useful for towers which have a large explosion range, such as the Mortar.
    • The Rocketeer can be effective against the Hidden Boss because of its hidden detection and high DPS.
  • You can use the Frost Blaster, Freezer and Warden to slow the Hidden Boss down.
    • While Sledger and Tesla do not have hidden detection, they can still indirectly hit the Hidden Boss if a detectable enemy is near it, though this should not be relied on as its quite inconsistent.
    • Due to the Hidden Boss's size, various forms of stalling like freeze or knockback will be less effective on it.

Trivia

  • When the Uber Mystery was in the game, it had a chance of spawning the Hidden Boss, upon its death, in Golden and Fallen modes.
  • The current appearance of the Hidden Boss is very similar to the original version of the Shadow Boss.
  • On some devices, the Hidden Boss may have transparent skin instead of black skin, with certain graphics settings.
  • The cape used by the legacy Hidden Boss is a re-textured version of the Guardian of the Night Cape accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • Multiple Hidden Bosses can be seen as a chess piece in the Chess Board map, representing the Bishop chess piece.
    • One of the Hidden Bosses can also be seen hanging on the chess piece.

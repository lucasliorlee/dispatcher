It is not accessible anywhere else in the game, aside from in Sandbox Mode. This page will attempt to include information about both the version before removal and its re-added version.

Description

The Snowman was an enemy that exclusively appeared in Cold Ambush as part of the Violent Night Event. Currently, it can appear in Sandbox mode. It had a below average speed, but it had a high amount of health.

The Snowman could be spawned by the Snow Golem, which could make it a big threat early on.

Appearance

The Snowman is mainly made out of snow. Its rig has a waist. On its head there is an orange carrot and two black pebbles, which resembles eyes and a nose. On its torso, it has two large black pebble-buttons. There is also a slightly smaller pebble-button on its waist.

Event Strategy

As this enemy only appears through Sandbox mode, no strategy section is made for its current version. Instead, a strategy section for the one that appeared without Sandbox mode in Cold Ambush is listed instead.

  • Towers like the Golden Scout, Cowboy, Golden Cowboy, Militant or Crook Boss can take these down easily early on.
    • By the time Snow Golems spawn, stronger towers like the Minigunner, Golden Minigunner, Accelerator or Engineer are recommended to fend off against them.
  • As these enemies spawn in large numbers, towers capable of damaging multiple enemies at once, such as the Executioner and the Mortar can be useful against this.
    • This can be especially useful against the Snowmen spawned by the Snow Golems, as three spawn when a Snow Golem is defeated.

Description

The Breaker4 is an enemy that appears in Casual, Intermediate, Molten, Fallen, Challenge Trials and PVP modes. It has a decent amount of health, average speed and 35% defense. Upon death, it will spawn three Breaker3s which are invulnerable for a short period of time. These have less health and defense but are faster and can spawn one Breaker2 upon death, which in turn can spawn one Breaker upon death. This will create huge hordes of enemies, which can easily overwhelm unprepared players.

The Breaker4 can/could be spawned by the Mystery Summoner, Necromancer Boss and Void Reaver if the map has the Enemies Beta modifier. The Grave Digger also used to summon it in Easy mode on maps with the Enemies Beta modifier. Otherwise, all these enemies will spawn the Mystery Boss instead. The Breaker4 replaces the Mystery Boss in any map with the Enemies Beta modifier active.

The Breaker4 also appeared as part of The Classic Event and The Takeover Event. The Breaker4 was replaced by the Png Bot4 in Headache Headquarters.

Appearance

ModernLegacy

The Breaker4 is a humanoid with a waist, lower arms and lower legs in its rig. Its torso, waist and legs are purple with a star pattern of varying sizes, orientations and colors of white, pink and dark magenta. Its arms and head are made out of light purple construction paper, with the front and back faces of each part being separate halves held together by small strips of tape. It has four small red humanoids sticking out of its right shoulder, chest, upper right leg and lower left leg, all of which represent the Breaker3.

It plays a low-pitched squeaking sound and emits small waves and purple confetti particles on death.

The legacy Breaker4 has a pastel magenta skin color. It has a dark magenta torso and legs with a plaid stitch mark pattern on them. It has a red humanoid on its chest, representing the Breaker3.

It plays a crumbling sound on death.

Strategy

  • The Breaker4 spawns three Breaker3s upon death. This can create large hordes of enemies, which can make the Breaker4 quite a dangerous enemy.
    • As these spawn in hordes, area of effect towers like the Assassin, Mortar, Rocketeer, Mercenary Base's Grenadiers or Pursuit can be used to clear them out easily.
      • Towers which deal splash damage can be especially useful, as majority of them bypass defense.
    • Setting some towers to Strongest targeting can massively help against the Breaker4 as it avoids the Breaker3's (and its subsequent children's) invulnerability frames.
    • They can be especially threatening in maps with the All Paths modifier, which increases the amount of them that spawns significantly.
    • High DPS towers are also recommended, supported by the DJ Booth, Medic and Commander.
    • The Hacker can also be quite useful—especially in harder gamemodes and Challenge Trials—as it can prevent the Break ability from occurring when the Breaker4 is killed while targeted by the Hacker's range.
  • Be aware that you do not earn any cash from damaging enemies that the Breaker4 spawns in every mode.
  • The enemies that spawn from the Breaker4 do not need to be killed for wave progression to occur.

Trivia

  • The Breaker4 has the second most gamemode appearances, appearing in ten gamemodes total, behind the Breaker, Breaker2 and Breaker3.
  • The functionality of this enemy is very similar to the Solar Shard, Lunar Shard and Dust from the Solar Eclipse Event.
    • The appearance of this enemy parallels the Mystery Boss.
  • Curiously, the Breaker4's design features an extra Breaker3 figure, despite only spawning three of them with its ability.
  • The legacy Breaker4, along the Breaker, Breaker2 and Breaker3, will turn into a opaque recolored version of itself when turned into a unit by the Hacker.
  • While the Legacy version of the Breaker4 can drop a Logbook, getting it will only give the player the modern Breaker4 in the Index.

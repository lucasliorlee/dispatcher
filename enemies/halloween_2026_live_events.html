Description

Halloween 2026 Live Events, also known as Admin Abuse Live Events, were live events named after the celebration they released for.

The live event started on the 25th of September 2026 and lasted an hour, however the main event mode would run until the 28th of September 2026. It is planned to have a rerun on the 2nd of October 2026.

Live Events

First Live Event

The live event started in the Main Lobby. Once the countdown concluded, players would be teleported to Nil Zone II and the camera would pan toward the event entrance. Afterward, the remainder of the event would take place in Nil Zone II and a permanent portal would be available in the lobby to use at any time to start matches in Nil Zone II, allowing match sizes of Squads and Mega. In the map, the developers would admin abuse just like what they did for the Halloween 2025 Live Events except with different commands.

Some known admin abuses for this event were:

  • Placing random towers around the entire map, including towers not in any player's loadout and assigning ownership to random players of those new towers.
  • Making tacos rain from the sky, while playing Parry Gripp & BooneBum - Raining Tacos. Each taco collected would heal the base.
  • Money rain where each cash projectile collected would give the player a lot of Cash.
  • Reducing the visibility of players within a small radius and the fog would be pitch black, while also slowing down players.
  • Changing the positions of existing towers, including those placed by players, sometimes with a bouncy animation.

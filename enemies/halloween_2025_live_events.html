Description

Halloween 2025 Live Events, also known as Admin Abuse Live Events, were live events named after the celebration they released for.

The live event started on the 4th of October 2025 and lasted an hour and 15 minutes. It would then rerun with minor changes a week later on the 11th of October 2025, where it would last seven hours instead.

Live Events

First Live Event

The live event started in the event area, where the whole area started breaking down before changing to a black screen with Lord Exo talking over it:

"Laughs I've wondered. What happens when someone learns the true scope of their existence? To tear down that wall of illusion. The realization of how fragile their reality truly is. Behind the veil, I've waited, and tonight, the boundary between your world and mine shatters."

After this, everyone would be sent to the Nil Zone and the area would have a permanent portal in it to replay the mode for the duration of the live event.

The rest of the live event took place inside of the Nil Zone, where developers would admin abuse. Generally, in-universe, this would be done by Lord Exo, as it would pick up a keyboard that created a pop-up with the effect. Dialogue would also occasionally play to prepare the player or global chat messages that did the same, though through developers instead. This only lasted 30 minutes. These are all the known admin abuses:

  • Summoning in units, such as Mark II, Monster and War Machine Sentry. All of them would be listed as sent by Player.
  • Giving players cash, specifically $67,000 and $323,232 cash.
  • Making player perform emotes like Monster Mash and Frozen.
  • Playing Spooky!
  • Have Lord Exo send unique dialogue.
  • Set the base health to various values like 32 or 0.
  • Players were sped up.
  • Activate various modifiers, like Healthy Enemies, Flying Enemies, Broke, Birthday, Hidden Enemies, but also ones unique to the live event like Random Tower Positions and Tanky Enemies.

Afterwards, the portal remained open for the next few minutes before getting closed and replaced by another countdown timer.

Beating Nil Zone would have the following dialogue play:

"Lay in the comfort of your fallacy. It won't be long until it crumbles. The hour draws near when everything you cherish will be reduced to a memory. We will meet again very, very soon."

Second Live Event

The live event started in the event area, where the whole area started breaking down before changing to a black screen. Lord Exo was supposed to talk, however, similarly to the first live event, this did not play. However, unlike it, the opening sequence was not rerun with a fix, meaning it is unknown what was actually said here.

The rest of the live event took place inside of the Nil Zone, where developers would admin abuse. Generally, in-universe, this would be done by Lord Exo, as it would pick up a keyboard that created a pop-up with the effect. Dialogue would also occasionally play to prepare the player or global chat messages that did the same, though through developers instead. This only lasted around an hour. These are all the known admin abuses:

  • Various game events.
    • Money Rain, where Cash started raining down from the sky. It gave anyone who picked it up $100.
    • Raining Tacos, where either good tacos fell down from the sky that healed the base health by 5 or evil tacos which instead, damaged it by 5.
    • Alien Invasion, where a giant UFO would attack maps and the lobby with a giant laser beam, killing anything hit by it.
    • Pop Tart Rain, where pop tarts started falling from the sky while Nyan Cat surrounded the map. These gave $100 when picked up.
  • Players were given gears like the Gravity Coil, Turkey Leg or Subspace Tripmine.
  • Enemies like the Toilet, Fallen King and Grave Digger were spawned in.
  • Towers were placed down by the developers and assigned to random players. This includes the Slasher, Farm, War Machine and Mecha Base.
  • Snowballer was given to certain players as a sixth tower.
  • The Molten Monster was summoned.
  • Modifiers like Tanky Enemies, Healthy Enemies and Random Tower Positions were applied.
  • Played various songs, which includes Spooky!, 2024 Halloween Lobby Theme, Solar Eclipse Lobby, Skibidi and Gold Titan.
  • Random dialogue played, either from safazi or Lord Exo
  • Selling all towers.

Afterwards, the portal remained open for the next few hours before getting closed and replaced by another countdown timer.

Beating Nil Zone would have the following dialogue play:

"Every unknowing step serves my purpose. Each pawn plays their part. When the untouchable fall before me in paradise, who is there to stand in my way?"

Trivia

  • These Live Events were the second and third Live Events in Tower Defense Simulator, the first being the Hardcore Live Event.
  • This event could be played with up to 6 people.

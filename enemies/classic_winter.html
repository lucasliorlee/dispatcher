Classic Winter

Log in to edit
  • View history
  • Purge
Sandbox Only Content

Classic Winter was previously removed from the game on the 28th of May 2024 as it was part of the The Classic Event, though it has been made available through the Sandbox mode.
It is not accessible anywhere else in the game, aside from in Sandbox Mode. This page will attempt to include information about both the version before removal and its re-added version.

Description

Classic Winter was the second map of The Classic Event. It has been made available again through the Sandbox mode. Like other event maps, this map has a specialized wave structure with special enemies. Triumphing this map rewarded the player with 1 Token. There was also 1 Tix on the map, which could be found left of the enemy entrance.

This map has a premade loadout. It consists of the Military Base, Militant, Freezer, Crook Boss and Sniper. Skins and golden perks could not be applied to these towers without the use of bugs, though this is not the case in Sandbox mode.

This map had four modifiers. The first was Enemies Beta, which changed the appearance of some enemies. The second was Classic Roblox, which activated the next two modifiers on the map. The third modifier aws Classic Oof, which made enemies play the old Roblox death sound. The fourth was Retro Death, which made enemies collapse into multiple pieces upon death. When activated in Sandbox mode, only Classic Oof and Retro Death are applied.

Design & Path

Classic Winter takes place in a snowy, flat area surrounded by hills. Multiple cliffs can be found on the map, which act as cliff spots for towers. There are trees scattered across the map. A few houses can also be seen on the map.

The brown path begins and ends at two snow caves at two of the snow mounds. The path takes a long straight before doubling back on itself twice.

The map slowly corrupted itself, turning parts of the map into a dark black color, and making other objects float in the sky. This no longer happens in Sandbox mode.

Event Rewards

The game calculated how many Coins and EXP you receive based on how many waves you survived and if you won a match. During periods of 100% more EXP (Friday – Sunday), any EXP earned was multiplied by 2x through the XP Boost modifier. The VIP gamepass also multiplied any EXP earned by 1.25x through the VIP Boost modifier.

Any rewards that ended in a decimal point were rounded down, no matter the first figure of the leading decimal.

Rewards were calculated as:

Coins Reward EXP Reward
( 100 ÷ (1 + [(12 ÷ Current Wave) - 1]^1.3) + 100 (Triumph Only)) × 1.3 ( 30 EXP ÷ (1 + [(12 ÷ Current Wave) - 1])) × 1.3

Strategy

  • The Military Base is very effective on this map due to its units having high DPS, high HP, and its Airstrike ability.
    • In fact, you can just use the Military Base alone and it will be able to beat this map.
  • The Militant can be useful against enemies that spawn on this map, due to its decent DPS.

Trivia

  • This map is based on the first version of Winter, a map that has been replaced by Winter Abyss.

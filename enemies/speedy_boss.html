Description

The Speedy Boss, previously known as the Boss3, is a large and fast enemy that appears in Casual, Challenge Trials, Legacy Molten and Intermediate modes. It can also appear in Basic Arena PVP, but there it is only seen if sent by a player. It has less health than the Slow Boss, but it makes up for that in its speed, having the same speed as the Hidden. The Speedy Boss has no immunities.

The Speedy Boss can be spawned from the Mystery Boss upon its death in Legacy Molten mode.

Appearance

ModernLegacy

The current version of the Speedy Boss is a hefty humanoid with a waist and lower arms in its rig. It has a cyan blue skin color as well as a dark brown torso, waist and legs. It wears black braces with white chains attached. It is faceless and its main body is lightly covered in dirt.

It wears a grey vest on its body with a glowing light blue lightning bolt emblem that has a shining particle. The front side of the vest has a blue stripe going down the middle of the vest with smaller stripes leading up towards the backside. The backside of the vest has an exposed compartment with a red switch and two batteries labeled "Durablox" embedded into a battery compartment. It also has two vents on the backside that emit a large blue aura. It constantly emits sparking and lightning particles.

The current version of the legacy Speedy Boss has a light blue skin color. It has a blue granite torso, legs and skull shoulder-pads. It has blue diamond-plated armbands, and blue sparks are emitted from its arms. It has a glowing blue lightning bolt emblem on its torso, as well as sharp glowing blue spikes growing out of its shoulders. Additionally, it has a light blue face mask covering its facial area, which lacks a face.

The current Enemies Beta version of the legacy Speedy Boss is a hefty humanoid with a waist and lower arms in its rig. It has a dark blue skin color, as well as a dark brown torso, waist and legs. The ends of its limbs are covered in a blue fluid. It has a glowing blue lightning bolt emblem on its torso, along with diagonally opposite glowing cuts on the sides of the torso. It has no facial features and it wears black armbands with grey spikes.

The fourth version of the legacy Speedy Boss had a light blue skin color. It hads a blue granite torso and legs. It had blue diamond-plated armbands. It had a glowing blue lightning bolt emblem on its torso, as well as sharp glowing blue spikes growing out of its shoulders. Additionally, it has two diagonal scars that form a mirrored "y" symbol".

The third version of the legacy Speedy Boss is similar to the fourth version, except it had a drooling zombie face instead with glowing yellow eyes.

The second version of the legacy Speedy Boss is similar to the fourth version, except it had a lighter blue pebble torso and legs. It also had thinner armbands, and its crystals were a darker blue and did not glow as much. It also had a drooling zombie face with glowing yellow eyes.

The first version of the legacy Speedy Boss, then called Boss3, had a desaturated yellow skin color, as well as a golden marble torso and legs. It had a drooling zombie face with yellow eyes, as well as an emissive yellow top hat with a purple band. It also had a shiny golden tie and had yellow pulses and beams emitting from its torso.

Strategy

  • Early-game towers like the Militant, Toxic Gunner, Cowboy, Ace Pilot and Military Base can do quite well against this.
    • DPS towers like the Accelerator, Minigunner or Ranger can also be used, though they are not necessary, especially in easier modes.
  • As the Speedy Boss is quite fast, stalling towers like the Freezer, Electroshocker, Warden or Brawler can be used to slow it down.
    • Do note that its size will make it affected less by various forms of stalling, such as freeze or knockback.

Trivia

  • The original name and appearance of the Speedy Boss was based on the Boss3 from Tower Battles.
  • When the Uber Mystery was in the game, it had a chance of spawning the Speedy Boss upon its death in Golden and Fallen modes.
  • The Speedy King can be considered as a Hardcore variant of the Speedy Boss.
  • The skull shoulder pads used by the fifth appearance of the Speedy Boss are re-textured versions of the The Riddling Skull accessory from the Roblox catalog.
  • Despite being a boss version of the Speedy, the third and fourth versions of the Speedy Boss looked more similar to the Quick than the Speedy.
  • Oddly, in v1.23.0, the appearance of the Speedy Boss was not updated to always use the Enemies Beta appearance like how other Molten mode enemies were.
    • This is most likely due to the fact that the Speedy Boss did not appear in Easy mode at the time, since the Molten mode enemies that had their appearances changed also appeared in Easy mode.
  • The "32" writing on the side-end of the Speedy Boss's metal booster is a reference to the 32 meme within the Tower Defense Simulator community.
  • The word "Durablox" written on the batteries is a portmanteau of Duracell, which is a real-life battery brand, and Roblox.

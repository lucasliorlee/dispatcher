Description

Nightmare Carnival was once a normal map in rotation, but it was an event map during the Halloween 2019 Event.

Like other event maps, Nightmare Carnival had a specialized wave structure with special enemies. Upon triumphing in the event version, players were rewarded with the Shredder. The reward on this map was the same as Fallen mode rewards at the time up to Wave 20.

In the event version of Nightmare Carnival, players started off with $750 (or $850 if players were in the community group). You could only play this map if you were Level 10+. Upon triumph, you received 360 and 50 EXP. Additionally, during the event, shared cash was 50%.

After the Halloween 2019 Event had ended, the players were not able to get special reward for triumphing this map.

Design & Path

Version 2Version 1

The second version of Nightmare Carnival was a night-time map that took place in mountainous terrain, though poisoned water surrounded part of the map. Various circus tents, haybales, dead trees, lanterns and pumpkins could be seen around the map. Various buildings could also be seen. A rollercoaster, drop tower and ferris wheel could be seen on this map. A spider was seen by the gate.

The second version of Nightmare Carnival was a two-pathed map, so enemies alternated between the spawn points. The two paths started at two separate caves. One path passed underneath an archway and through the gate. Both paths crossed two wooden bridges over a murky pond and intersected just after the bridges. The path then crossed over two wooden bridges before reaching the end.

The first version of Nightmare Carnival (the same one used for the Halloween 2019 Event) had Halloween themes spread throughout the map. It was a night-time map though the map itself was brightly illuminated by the attractions. It appeared to take place in a graveyard with a visiting circus. Many background houses, caves and gates were spread throughout the map. There was a drop tower ride, which was animated, with circus tents around the map. Pumpkins, windmills, lantern lights and banners could be seen throughout the map.

The path began and ended at two caves. The path itself was quite long, revolving around a large hill, then revolving around the drop tower, running nearly parallel to the fourth straight at one point before twisting around to the end.

Story

The Umbra in her search of more God Cube fragments detects one in Nightmare Carnival, so she sends Jack O' Bot to collect it[1]. The T.D.S. Organization dispatches their agents to defeat Jack O' Bot, successfully defending the carnival. It is unclear if the God Cube fragment was taken by the T.D.S. Organization, snatched by someone else from The Umbra's army or was just left there in Nightmare Carnival.

Event Rewards

The game calculated how much Coins and EXP you receive based on how many waves you survived and if you won a match.

Any rewards that end in a decimal point were rounded down, no matter the first figure of the leading decimal.

The rewards during the event were calculated as:

Coins Reward EXP Reward
18 × Wave Waves Completed × 2.5 EXP

($70 - 7 × (Players - 1)) × Waves Completed

Strategy

  • There are very few ideal cliff spots on this map.
    • The few suitable cliff towers are rather spaced out, making it hard to boost all cliff towers with only one support tower, like the Commander.
  • Ground towers can be placed near the circus tents as they can hit enemies on both paths from here.
  • Towers with long range, like the Turret, are highly recommended on this map.
    • The DJ Booth can help increase the range of towers.
  • Remember that, if you use the Military Base, only one unit will spawn from it, which will go down a random path. The same thing applies to the Crook Boss's body guards.

Trivia

  • Prior to Nightmare Carnival being officially added, a picture of the map was seen in the lobby.
    • However, there was no event elevator until the 18th of October 2019.
  • This was the second event map to be re-added as a regular map, with the first being The Heights from the SFOTH Event.
  • Like every other two-pathed map, all enemies (including major bosses) used to spawn twice prior to the Frost Update. Enemies then alternated in spawn points between the paths after the Frost Update.
    • Both Nightmare Carnival and Candy Valley were the only two-pathed maps with a rating of Hard instead of Insane.
  • A statue of the Reaper could be seen on one of the cliffs in the second version of this map.
  • Act II of the Lunar Overture Event is a retelling of the events that happened in the Halloween 2019 Event on this map, with a lot of enemies from this map being remade for the event. The first form of The Haunted Past also resembled this map.

Description

Tutorial is the map used as the tutorial for the game. For the introductory stage of the game, you are forced to complete at least the first mission on Story mode's TDS: Boot Camp chapter, called Boot Camp. Subsequent TDS: Boot Camp missions, Live Fire, Breach Protocol and Brute Force also use this map.

Design & Path

CurrentVersion 1

Tutorial is a green block with a few trees, a wooden watchtower, slightly elevated green ground and a road.

The path starts at the grey spawn point and ends at the red spawn point.

Tutorial took place in a grassy plains surrounded by mountains. There were also a few cliffs on the map and a black road that acted as the enemy path.

The path started and ended at a tunnel.

Rewards

The Tutorial used to give out 600 and 200 EXP upon first triumph, though due to its tutorial being incorporated into the Boot Camp, this is no longer the case.

Badges

There is also one badge which can be obtained when playing on this map. It is:

Welcome to TDS!
✔ OBTAINABLE
You completed the tutorial! Welcome to Tower Defense Simulator recruit!


20,669,973 players earned this

Strategy

  • Because of how Tutorial works, simply following the Commander can lead to triumphing this map.
    • It is not possible to pick what towers are in your loadout, as they are replaced the moment the match starts. Additionally, all skins are replaced with the Default skin and golden perks are not applied.

Trivia

  • There was an image of Tung Tung Tung Sahur on one of the trees, which ended up getting replaced by the Golden Snowballer's icon in v2.7.0.
  • Despite the objective screen suggesting triumphing the Tutorial gave the Demoman, this was not actually the case. The player would buy it with the Coins gotten from triumphing it instead.

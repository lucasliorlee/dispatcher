Description

Bloated is a status effect that for most of its uses, doubles an enemy's health.

Mechanics

Bloated enemies will have their health be increased by 100% in most circumstances, though rarely the buff will be a different percentage. This only affects their regular health, Shield Health is not increased. Their model size is also visually increased by 50%.

The Healthy Enemies modifier gives every enemy Bloated. Similarly, all enemies in the Juggernaut challenge map spawn with the Bloated attribute due to the Juggernaut modifier, although it should be noted that some enemies' health values are altered.

Enemies can receive Bloated, even if they did not spawn with it if they are hit by a gift explosion created by the Fallen Jester's Devious Tricks ability and Hex Weaver's Spooky Tricks ability. The Protein Shake consumable also applies Bloated to the next three enemies sent by a player. In the Null & Void Event, if the Death curse was selected, all enemies would spawn with Bloated.

Update History

  • 3 May 2024 - v1.20.0
    • Bloated added.
    • Bug: Units deal damage at a really fast rate while not being damaged themselves when a Bloated enemy has less than half health.
    • Bug: Bloated enemies do not generate cash when damaged in legacy modes if the enemy has less than half health.
    • Bug: Bloated does not increase the health of enemies if they had their health altered by modifiers.
    • Bug: Bloated enemies will sometimes show incorrect max health after scalin.
  • 23 June 2024 - v1.23.1
    • Bugfix: Unit now deal damage to Bloated enemies correctly.
    • Bugfix: Health increased from Bloated now generates cash.
  • 2 August 2024 - v1.27.0
    • Bloated now makes enemies bigger by anywhere between 15% and 25% through Scale.
  • 9 August 2024 - v1.27.5
    • Bugfix: Bloated now increases health of enemies that had their health altered by modifiers.
  • 30 October 2024 - v1.39.0
    • Bloated size increased from anywhere between 15% and 25% to 50%.
  • 5 June 2026 - v2.0.0
    • Bugfix: Enemies now show correct max health after Bloated scales it.

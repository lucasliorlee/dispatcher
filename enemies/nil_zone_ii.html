Description

Nil Zone II is the the map for the Halloween 2026 Live Events. Just like other event maps, this map has a specialized wave structure with special enemies. Players started with a varying amount of cash, based on the difficulty chosen. It had no unique rewards other than the Banned Crate, which can only be gotten every 24 hours from triumphs. Logbooks and Tower XP gain are disabled on this map.

Cash is only rewarded if an enemy was killed, or in some cases, damaged by a specific amount of health. Additionally, the amount gained is a set amount of cash, which is not always equal to the enemy's health. Enemies split their cash between players, regardless of how much damage the player dealt to it. If, in a duo, one player did all the damage to an enemy, both players would receive 50% of the cash the enemy would normally give in solo. This means that in solo, you received 100% of the cash when an enemy is killed, or it was damaged enough. With each additional player, this is reduced to 50% in duo, 33% in trio and 25% in quad. If it is a decimal number, it got rounded up or down depending on the decimal value. If it was at least 0.5, it gets rounded up. If it was less than 0.5, it got rounded down.

Design & Path

Nil Zone II takes place on a desolate corrupted area in the middle of nowhere. It is made out of purple Roblox parts with studs and purple binary on them. This area is surrounded by multiple pillars, which either have moving orange cogs on them or giant hands. Behind the entrance, there is also a large tower placed on nothing with a cog at the front of it.

The path starts and ends at a white portal. The map takes a short straight path which leads into two u-turns, ending with another short straight path that leads to the exit.

Preset Loadouts

Each player was allowed to pick from one of three preset loadouts. Alongside these preset options, players could opt to use their current loadout as the first choice. However, if one's loadout contained no towers, this option would be unavailable.

Tactician
A well-rounded and adaptable loadout.


Crowd Control
A class that focuses on debuffing and AOE damage.


Specialist
A class that uses unique towers that excel at specific roles.


Rewards

The game calculated how many Coins and EXP you receive based on how many waves you survived and if you won a match. During periods of 100% more EXP (Friday – Sunday), any EXP earned is multiplied by 1.5x through the XP Boost modifier. The VIP gamepass also multiplied any EXP earned by 1.25x through the VIP Boost modifier. These two boosts were additive.

Strategy

  • Splash towers capable of dealing with crowd control such as Demoman, Rocketeer, Pulse Trooper and Mortar can be quite useful given the path layout and the considerably high health of enemies in the early waves.
    • Alternatively, towers that can stun enemies such as Electroshocker and Tesla are also valuable.
  • Having two players with DJ Booth, although not required, is recommended given the large amounts of enemies that may cluster through those two paths connected to each other.
  • If not playing in multiplayer, do not choose the Tactician loadout as a preset as it basically makes you start off with $1,200. ($1,300 if in the Paradoxum Games official group.). And all towers in the loadout are more expensive then the amount of cash given. The player would be entirely helpless and would not be able to place any tower, and there is no option for skipping waves, which results in an instant death on the first wave itself. DJ Booth will be practically useless as it does not deal damage until Level 3.

Trivia

  • Nil Zone II is the first event map to utilize Evolved towers in the preset loadouts.
  • Nil Zone II is nearly identical to that of Nil Zone—especially with its pathway—except that the path was slightly adjusted to go through other paths which the first one did not apply, and that it is also more visible than Nil Zone.
    • Additionally, the map was redesigned from Nil Zone to more closely match the Halloween 2026 event rather than the Null & Void Event.

Description

Bathroom Approach was the event map played in the Ducky Revenge Event and in its Spring Frenzy Battle Pass rerun. Like other event maps, this map had a specialized wave structure with special enemies. Players started with a varying amount of cash, based on how many players there were in the match and the difficulty chosen. Triumphing the Easy difficulty did not grant any additional rewards, but it did unlock the Hard difficulty. Triumphing the Hard difficulty gives the Biologist tower on the first triumph, and in the additional rewards after their countdown timers finished.

Cash was only gained if an enemy is killed, or in some cases, damaged by a specific amount of health. Additionally, the amount gained was a set amount of cash, which was not always equal to the enemy's health. It used a slightly different method of distributing cash between players, as enemies split their cash between players, regardless of how much damage the player dealt to it. If, in a duo, one player did all the damage to an enemy, both players would receive 50% of the cash the enemy would normally give in solo. This means that in solo, you received 100% of the cash when an enemy is killed, or it was damaged enough. With each additional player, this was reduced to 50% in duo, 33% in trio and 25% in quad. If it was a decimal, it gets rounded up or down depending on the decimal value. If it was at least 0.5, it got rounded up. If it was less than 0.5, it got rounded down. For example, the Ducky D00M III in the Hard difficulty gave $150,000 on death in solo, $75,000 in duo, $49,500 in trio and $37,500 in quad.

This map had the Path Spawn Even modifier, meaning enemies only spawned once. During the Ducky Revenge Event, it instead had the All Paths modifier, which duplicated every enemy except Ducky D00M III and Nerd Ducky for every path, but this has been removed for its current version.

In the hard difficulty only, the health of enemies is increased by 20% in duo and 30% in quad. During the Ducky Revenge Event, there was also a 6% Mutation, though this has since been removed. Mutation increased the health of enemies by 6% every wave after their debut.

Design & Path

Bathroom Approach was a giant bathroom located in the duck realm, with the actual map taking place on top of a large countertop. The countertop had multiple wooden blocks, used to create various objects. There were also multiple huge versions of regular items, such as cups, brushes, band aids, nail clippers, playing cards, potted plants, scissors and toothbrushes. Outside the countertop, there was also a giant toilet, bathtub, door, washing machine and shelf. On the ground, there was a giant fortress with multiple wooden halls leading to the countertop. In the fortress, Ducky D00M III and a giant robot mech were being built by ducks.

The path started at two giant wooden halls and ended at a small house built out of blocks. There were two paths in Easy and four in Hard.

Rewards

Only contains current rewards, so ones from the Spring Frenzy Battle Pass. Past rewards can be found in its Rewards subpage.

The game calculated how many Coins and EXP you received based on how many waves you survived and if you won a match. During periods of 100% more EXP (Friday – Sunday), any EXP earned was multiplied by 2x through the XP Boost modifier. The VIP gamepass also multiplied any EXP earned by 1.25x through the VIP Boost modifier. These two boosts were additive.

Rewards were calculated as:

800 ÷ (1 + [(25 ÷ Current Wave) - 1]^1.3) + 200 (Triumph Bonus) 200 EXP ÷ (1 + [(25 ÷ Current Wave) - 1]) + 31 EXP (Triumph Bonus)

The second triumph reward had to be done 64 hours after the first one, while the third one had to be done 64 hours after the second triumph.

Bunny Sentry

Bunny Sentries were an interactable which could be bought on this map. These were small turrets which look like bunnies and shoot at enemies. These had 300 damage, 0.275 firerate, 25 range, flying detection and hidden detection. Their targeting was also set to Strongest.

The first Bunny Sentry costed $75,000 to build, and every Bunny Sentry afterwards costed $50,000 more.

On Wave 25, the Nerd Ducky would do a supercharged attack that deals damage to the base health. These could be used to stop this from happening, as the Nerd Ducky would not deal damage to the base health if they were built, it would instead destroy one.

Wave Structure

The formula for calculating wave bonus was based on the current wave. Between Wave 1 to 15, it was:

$600 + ([Waves Completed - 1] × $100)^1.13

Between Wave 16 to 25 in Hard, it is:

$600 + ([Waves Completed - 1] × $100)^1.2

Depending on player count, the wave bonus was reduced using multipliers. These multipliers were:

Players Reduction per Player Total Reduction
Solo 0% 0%
Duo 3% 6%
Trio 3% 9%
Quad 3% 12%

The wave clear bonus appears when every enemy from that wave was killed. It was 40% of the wave bonus in solo, while for duo, trio and Quad it was 20%.

The waves in Bathroom Approach could be skipped.

Strategy

  • While there are many threatening waves, in many cases the enemies which are the dangerous part of it only spawn on one part of the map.
    • In Hard, more notable waves include:
      • On Wave 8 and 9, multiple Flying Duckies will spawn but only on the green and yellow paths.
      • On Wave 10, P3NGU will spawn only on the green path.
      • On Wave 15, the Mega Ducky will spawn only on the blue path.
      • On Wave 20, Ducky D00M III will spawn only on the orange path.
      • On Wave 25, the Nerd Ducky will spawn only on the yellow path.
  • Decent starting towers like the Cowboy, Golden Cowboy, Engineer, Ace Pilot or Golden Soldier can be used.
  • This map features a lot of Flying and Hidden enemies, meaning you need strong hidden detection and flying detection defenses against them.
    • Towers with both of these detections, such as the Golden Crook Boss, Ace Pilot or Executioner can be particularly useful here because of this.
    • The Ace Pilot and Firework Technician can be used to give towers which normally do not have both detections, such as the Ranger, Mortar or Pursuit both detections, making it far easier to deal with these enemies.
  • This map has paths which are separated from each other, essentially requiring two separate two different sets of defenses for each side.
    • While the Pursuit can only patrol one side at at time, it can easily change between sides with its Patrol ability, making it pretty useful here.
      • It is also especially useful on Wave 33 of the Hard difficulty, as it allows it to follow the Nerd Ducky as it jumps between paths.
    • The Ranger is pretty useful here, as while it cannot cover the entire map, it can cover a large chunk of it.
    • The DJ Booth is very useful here, as it can allow towers to cover a larger area of the map.
  • The Medic can be useful here, as enemies that stun towers start spawning from Wave 6 and as waves progress, become even more common.
    • The Mercenary Base's Field Medic can also be helpful, as most of these enemies can also damage units.
  • Bunny Sentries that appear on this map can be used as additional DPS, especially due to them having both flying and hidden detections, though they are not mandatory to win.
    • It is, however, recommended to get them before Wave 25, because if none are on the map, the Nerd Ducky will do a powerful attack that stuns towers, heavily damages units and deals 30 damage to the base health. Additionally, if Bunny Sentries are present during that attack, they will stun the Nerd Ducky giving you more time to kill it.
      • This is however not necessary if Skills are enabled, as the overheal and increased base health from it means you can tank every ability usage. It does however result in a stun, damage to units and the Nerd Ducky getting slightly further on the path, meaning there are drawbacks to ignoring the ability even if it will not kill you.

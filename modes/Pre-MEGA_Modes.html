Description

Before the MEGA Update, there were four main modes, these being the Easy, Normal, Hard and Insane modes. As these modes were nearly identical, with only the wave amount, wave bonus and wave cash being different, they were combined into one page on the wiki. In Pre-MEGA modes, shared cash was calculated as Damage ÷ Players. For example, if someone else damaged an enemy by 10 health in a server with two people, you would receive $5. However, in a server with three people, you would receive $3.

Before the 5th of August 2019, there was only one mode which had 56 waves; as it was not given a name, it will be referred to as Pre-MEGA mode.

Rewards

The game calculated how much Coins and EXP you received based on how many waves you survived.

Any rewards that end in a decimal point are rounded down, no matter the first figure of the leading decimal.

Wave Structure

In Pre-MEGA modes, the formulas for calculating wave bonus on each wave and mode were:

Mode Wave Bonus
Easy $60 x Waves Completed
Normal $63 x Waves Completed
Hard $66 x Waves Completed
Insane $72 x Waves Completed

Prior to the 5th of August 2019, Cash rewards were calculated as:

$60 x Waves Completed

Prior to the 3rd of August 2019, Cash rewards were calculated as:

$55 x Waves Completed

For a short period of time on the 2nd of August 2019, Cash rewards were calculated as:

$40 x Waves Completed

Prior to the 2nd of August 2019, Cash rewards were calculated as:

Player Count Wave Bonus
Solo $75 x Waves Completed
Duo $60 x Waves Completed
Trio $50 x Waves Completed
Quad+ $40 x Waves Completed

Prior to the 19th of July 2019, Cash rewards were calculated as:

Player Count Wave Bonus
Solo $75 x Waves Completed
Duo+ $40 x Waves Completed

Prior to the 14th of July 2019, Cash rewards were calculated as:

$40 x Waves Completed

Prior to the 10th of July 2019, Cash rewards were calculated as:

$25 x Waves Completed

Bolded names indicates an enemy type's debut.

For former wave structures of Pre-MEGA modes, visit the Waves page.

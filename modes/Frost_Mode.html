Description

Frost mode is one of the six main survival modes. Frost mode has 40 waves. It is named after snow and frost-themed enemies in said mode.

Cash is only gained if an enemy is killed, or in some cases, damaged by a specific amount of health. Additionally, the amount gained is a set amount of cash, which is not always equal to the enemy's health. It uses a slightly different method of distributing cash between players, as enemies split their cash between players, regardless of how much damage the player dealt to it. If, in a duo, one player does all the damage to an enemy, both players will receive 50% of the cash the enemy would normally give in solo. This means that in solo, you receive 100% of the cash when an enemy is killed, or it is damaged enough. With each additional player, this is reduced to 50% in duo, 33% in trio and 25% in quad. If it is a decimal, it gets rounded up or down depending on the decimal value. If it is at least 0.5, it gets rounded up. If it is less than 0.5, it gets rounded down.

The health of enemies in this mode scales with the player count, starting at two players. The increase 30% in duo, 45% in trio and 60% in quad. It is applied after attributes, like Bloated, meaning they can affect the health of an enemy. The increase is not always the stated amount.

Rewards

The game calculates how many Coins and EXP you receive based on how many waves you survived and if you won a match. During periods of 50% more EXP (Friday – Sunday), any EXP earned is multiplied by 1.5x through the XP Boost modifier. The VIP gamepass also multiplies any EXP earned by 1.25x through the VIP Boost modifier. These two boosts are additive. Some maps like Mason Arch can also increase all rewards by a specific amount. Regular EXP boosts are multiplicative with other modifiers.

Any rewards that end in a decimal point is rounded down, no matter the first figure of the leading decimal.

Rewards are calculated as:

Coins Reward Gem Reward EXP Reward Tower EXP Reward
800 ÷ (1 + [(40 ÷ Current Wave) - 1]^2.4) + 1,200 (Triumph Bonus) 45 (Triumph Bonus) 200 EXP ÷ (1 + [(40 ÷ Current Wave) - 1]^2) + 100 EXP (Triumph Bonus) 85 EXP ÷ (1 + [(40 ÷ Current Wave) - 1]) + 110 EXP (Triumph Bonus)

Wave Structure

Currently, Frost mode has two wave bonuses, based on the current wave. Between waves 1 to 30, the wave bonus is calculated as:

$325 + ([Waves Completed - 1] × $80)^1.03

Between waves 31 and 40, it is calculated as:

$325 + ([Waves Completed - 1] × $80)^1.07

Depending on player count, the wave bonus is reduced using multipliers. These multipliers are:

Players Reduction per Player Total Reduction
Solo 0% 0%
Duo 15% 30%
Trio 13% 39%
Quad 10% 40%

The wave clear bonus appears when every enemy from that wave was killed and no damage was dealt to the base health. It is 7.5% of the wave bonus in solo, while for duo, trio and quad it is 5%.

Trivia

  • Frost mode is a returning mode derived from Pre-MEGA Insane Mode.
  • Frost mode is the first main gamemode to feature Dispatcher and Professor V.
  • Wave 39 is likely a reference to the Wave 68 of Insane Mode.
    • The Frost Hero, Deep Freeze and Frozens spawn three, four and eight times on Wave 39 respectively. This is the same as Wave 68, where three Frost Heroes, four Deep Freezes and eight Frozens spawn.
    • Three Mega Frost Mysteries also spawn. While such enemies did not exist back then, the number spawned does matched the amount of Rusted that spawn on Wave 68, that being three.
  • Frost mode is not canon in the game's story, as mentioned by the developers multiple times.[20]
  • Frost mode is currently the only Survival mode without any permanent Achievements.

Description

The Elf Camp is a unit spawning tower that could have been unlocked by triumphing Cold Ambush during the Violent Night Event. After a player had returned to the lobby, it was claimed through the Gift Box menu. It could also have been obtained through a limited time gamepass for 500 during the Violent Night Event and Krampus' Revenge Event, and for 799 during the The Final Act Event.

The Elf Camp is a spawner tower that summons elves. These elves are modernized versions of the enemies that appeared in the Violent Night Event. At Level 0, it only summons the Elf. However, with later Levels, it can spawn multiple units at once instead of just one. The functionality of these units varies, making each unit unique. With Levels, some units will be replaced by their stronger variants.

For the first upgrade, the Elf Camp only summons Elves, which can only collide with enemies. This unit is later replaced with the Guardian Elf, which can also damage enemies with its sword. At Level 1, it can spawn an additional unit, the Snowball Elf, that throws snowballs at enemies from a distance, dealing damage. It is replaced by the Cannoneer Elf at Level 2, which deals more DPS, and by the Gunner Elf at Level 3, which fires in bursts and at Level 4 spawns twice, dealing even more DPS. At Level 2, it can also spawn the Bomber Elf, which explodes upon death, dealing damage to enemies in its explosion radius. It is replaced by the Gift Bomber at Level 4, which instead throws present bombs and flies in a sleigh, allowing it to avoid colliding with non-Boss enemies. It does not explode upon death. At Level 4, it can also spawn its final unit, the Ripped Elf, which throws a large snow boulder at enemies, dealing a large amount of damage to any caught in the explosion radius.

The Elf Camp is considerably an early to mid-game tower as its cheap cost and units such as the Snowball Elf or Bomber Elf can be quite useful in the early-game, while its later units like the Ripped Elf or Gunner Elf can be quite useful in the mid-game.

There is a limit of four Elf Camps per player.

Strategy

  • The Elf Camp can be an effective counter against defense enemies, as its units deal full damage when colliding with enemies.
    • Splash Damage from the Bomber Elf or Ripped Elf is also not affected by Defense.
    • Damage from the Guardian Elf and ranged elves like the Snowball Elf or Gunner Elf is still affected.
  • It is best advised to place the Elf Camp away from the main path, as it has no use close to it.
  • The Elf Camp can be very useful against Hidden enemies due to most of its units having hidden detection.
    • The Gift Bomber can also detect Flying enemies while the Elf Camp is at Level 4.
  • Do note that some enemies can damage units. As most of the Elf Camp units have low health, they will often be killed instantly.
  • Despite being a flying unit, the Gift Bomber is affected by enemy abilities that damage units, even if it does not look like was not hit by it.
  • The Bomber Elf's splash damage can be quite useful at clearing out hordes of enemies that appear early on.
    • Later on, the Ripped Elf and Gift Bomber can also prove useful for the stronger hordes of Slow Bosses, although it takes a while for the projectiles to hit, meaning they could miss faster enemies.
      • The Ripped Elf's snowballs may not hit the path most of the time.
  • The Electroshocker, Frost Blaster, Freezer and Toxic Gunner pair well with the Elf Camp as they slow down enemies before colliding with units, allowing the units to survive longer.
  • Units spawned by the Elf Camp are not affected by any buffs or debuffs.
  • Units spawned by the Elf Camp are far less effective against Ghost enemies as they cannot collide with them and only some of the units can detect them.
    • The Gift Bomber, despite being a flying unit, cannot collide with Flying enemies.
    • However, they are effective against Lead enemies, as collision and splash damage deals damage to them.
  • As the Gift Bomber is a flying unit, it cannot collide with most enemies. It can only collide with Boss enemies, like the Molten Warlord or Fallen King.
  • The two Gunner Elves that spawn are not one unit but two.
    • This means the DPS of both units is effectively doubled at Level 4.

Trivia

  • The elves spawned by this tower are based on multiple enemies that appear in the Violent Night Event.
    • The Elf is based on the Basic Elf.
    • The Bomber Elf is based on the enemy of the same name.
    • The Guardian Elf is based on the Cookie Guard
    • The Gift Bomber is based on the Sleigh.
    • The Ripped Elf is based on the enemy of the same name.
      • The Ripped Elf is the first humanoid unit to use a body similar to R15.
    • The Snowball Elf, Cannoneer Elf and Gunner Elf do not appear to be based on any enemies from the event.
      • Though the Gunner Elf does use the same accessories as the Builder Elf.
  • This is the first tower that can spawn multiple different units at once.
    • It is also the first event tower to spawn units.
  • The Level 2 upgrade cost is likely a reference to the year the Elf Camp was added in.

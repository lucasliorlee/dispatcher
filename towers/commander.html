Description

The Commander is an advanced support tower that decreases the firerate of towers within its range, increasing their damage output. It can be purchased in the store for 4,000.

The Commander provides a 10% firerate boost to towers in its range, making them attack enemies faster. This buff can be boosted further at Level 2+ through the Call to Arms ability, which increases it by 10% for ten seconds. Both buffs get higher with levels, with the passive buff getting increased to 20% and the Call to Arms ability buff getting increased to 20%. It also has a second ability, Support Caravan, which costs $2,000 to use. This will summon two Gunner APCs and one Missile APC on the map. When any ability used, it will enter fury mode, allowing it to attack any enemy in its range for ten seconds. The attack time from using an ability stacks if multiple are used at once or when an ability is used while it is attacking. When an ability is used while fury mode is active, the timer for it will stop until the ability animation is finished.

The Commander is the primary narrator in most matches. Text dialogue appears beside the image or the model with an idle animation of the Commander, corresponding to the appearance of certain waves. This dialogue appears regardless of whether a Commander tower is present on the map, and the image or model of the Commander next to the dialogue will display different emotions.

The Commander has a placement limit of six per player.

Strategy

  • Firerate boost is calculated as
    Original Firerate ÷ (1+Firerate Boost)
    where Original Firerate is the base firerate of the tower,
    Firerate Boost is the boost supplied by the Commander in decimal form (for example, 10% is 0.1).
    • For example, a Level 4 Commander (20%) will decrease the firerate of a tower firing from 1 second to 0.833 seconds.
      • While in the game it may look like the firerate is rounded down to the second decimal, this is only visual. The firerate is longer than that and is not rounded up.
    • The Commander can boost the pump time of the Enforcer, meaning it is fully effective on it if units are not included.
    • The Commander cannot decrease the cooldown of burst towers.
    • The Commander also cannot boost the Executioner due to the axe not returning in time to take advantage.
  • When placing a Commander, try to place it near as many towers as possible, as this will allow for a high number of towers to be boosted.
    • This is also true in reverse; it is recommended that you place your towers close to a Commander (if there is one) for the Firerate Buff.
    • This also includes cliff-based towers. The Ace Pilot and Pursuit can also be boosted, though their towers (runway and helipad, respectively) must be within range of the Commander.
  • Slow firing towers, such as the Rocketeer or Ranger, will benefit massively from the Firerate Buff that the Commander supplies.
  • Placing three Level 2+ Commanders and activating their Call to Arms ability one at a time every 10 seconds is one of the most effective ways to utilize the Commander, as Call to Arms will be permanently active if this is done correctly, providing the 20% buff permanently.
    • You can also place two Level 2+ Commanders and constantly sell and replace one to renew its Call to Arms ability to save a placement for another tower, or four if you want to be safer with keeping the Call to Arms ability active at all times while not having to worry about selling and replacing Commanders, as having three Commanders require you to be quite precise to keep the 20% buff at all times, and you may get distracted from using its ability while upgrading other towers.
    • There are a few ways to tell when to use another ability, which include waiting until the Firerate Buff icon and visuals disappears, waiting until the ability timer gets reduced by 10 (which without Skills, is it reaching 20) or waiting until the attack timer reaches 2.
  • You can increase the range in which the Commander can affect towers and the Call to Arms range by using the DJ Booth.
    • The DJ Booth can also be useful at reducing the usage cost of the Support Caravan ability.
  • It is best to use the Commander's Call to Arms ability on a boss with high health (such as a major boss, like the Fallen King).
    • Be aware that some bosses have blocking abilities, meaning that a wrongly timed Call to Arms ability will be useless.
    • Additionally, you cannot activate the Call to Arms ability if the Commander is stunned.
      • To overcome this, the Medic can be used to protect the Commander from stuns, though this is generally not recommended as the shield also provides boosts best used on other towers.
      • Alternatively, you can sell and replace the Commander. Do note that this will remove the passive Firerate Buff for a short while if there are no other Commanders placed down.
  • Be aware that you cannot stack the Call to Arms ability.
    • If a Call to Arms ability is still active for towers, then activating another Call to Arms will not grant any additional buff. However, it resets the duration of the ability.
    • However, the Call to Arms ability can stack with boosts from other towers, like the Gladiator's Warrior's Call.
  • The Commander cannot boost the firerate of the Ace Pilot's bombs, Pursuit's missiles, Commando's missiles or any units like the Railgun Tank from the Military Base or the Tommy Goon 2 from the Crook Boss, even if their associated tower is in the range of a Commander.
  • If the Call to Arms ability is active, and a new tower is placed in the range of the Commander, that tower will not be boosted by the ability until it is activated again.
  • While the Commander has flying, lead and hidden detection, it can only attack enemies with these attributes periodically with its two abilities. Given how costly it is to get its abilities and that they both have lengthy cooldowns, relying solely on the Commander to fight against enemies with these attributes is unwise.
    • Other towers like the Ace Pilot, Electroshocker and Militant are more effective and reliable against them, with the Commander buffing them.
  • When Call of Arms is used, the fireate buff is applied instantly, however, the displayed timer only appears once the animation is done, which is roughly 2 seconds. Therefore, to be more optimized with chaining the buff, wait until the timer hits 2, then activate the next ability.

Trivia

  • When the game was in Alpha, the Commander's upgrade stats were not displayed on its upgrade description. It was simply referred to as "sans support sans support".
  • The Commander is heavily inspired by the Tower Battles tower of the same name.
  • Before the Overhaul Update, the spawnrate of the Military Base could be boosted by a Commander (so that the spawn time between units is reduced), but it was through an unconventional method. The stated buff was subtracted from the spawnrate (an example is a 25% buff on a Level 5 Military Base; the spawnrate would decrease from 45 to 44.75).
    • As 44.75 seconds rounded up to 45 seconds, there was no noticeable decrease in spawn time.
    • However, this did not apply to the spawnrates of units from the Crook Boss.
      • Interestingly, the Call to Arms reduced the spawnrate of units from the Military Base further, but even this buff could not produce a noticeable decrease in spawnrate.
  • The original Call to Arms sound was just a short shout from Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2. This is the same sound used for the Gladiator's Warrior's Call ability.
    • The second version of the Call to Arms sounds were voiced by YouTuber JOHN ROBLOX. There were three lines voiced by him.
      • There were multiple other voice lines that were recorded by JOHN ROBLOX, however they were never added to the game. All of those lines, along with variations of current lines that were not added, can be heard here.
      • This version was replaced by Enthrallist, a developer for the game, on the 16th of May 2024 to match the quality of other voice lines at the time as well as to have a professional voice act the lines.
    • The Maid (and formerly the Eclipse) skin voice lines are voiced by nekoriru.
    • The Eclipse skin voice lines are voiced by AerelisVA.
    • The Umbra skin voice lines are voiced AerelisVA.
    • The Eggrypted skin voice lines were voiced by Eggrypted.
  • The Commander has a cat named Claudette, which can be seen in certain Story mode cutscenes, like Welcome Recruit. It was added to the game as a tribute to BelowNatural's and Maddy's first cat, which has passed away in early 2026.[7]
  • Originally, when the Commander was first added to the game, you could stand on the wooden stool before placing the Commander down.
  • Prior to the Community Maps Update, the Commander used to appear alongside the Pyromancer, Scout, Sniper and Minigunner on the "Triumph" screen. This was changed to your towers' loadout.
    • Before the Overhaul Update, it appeared alongside the Commando.
    • During Hardcore Testing BETA, it appeared alongside the Minigunner.
      • The "Triumph" screen used the Galactic skin instead of the Default skin.
    • Before the Solar Eclipse Update, it appeared alongside the Soldier.
  • Before the MEGA Update, multiple Call to Arms abilities could be stacked. This could reduce the firerate of towers that ordinarily had high, like the Outlaw, to low firerates. Due to most slow firing towers dealing a lot of damage in a single shot, this could easily kill major bosses, like the Gold Titan.
  • There was a poll made on BelowNatural's Twitter that held contestants for the next Golden skin. One of the options, which was the most chosen, was a Golden Commander skin.
    • Instead of a Golden Commander skin being made, there was instead a Golden Cowboy skin. It is unknown why this change was made.
  • The Commander enemy from Pizza Party is based on the Commander tower.
  • From Null Missile APC's logbook description, the APC was his first vehicle he ever commanded, since he was promoted.
  • Commander is a playable character in Paradoxum Games' second game, ALTER EGO.

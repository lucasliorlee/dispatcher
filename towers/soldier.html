Description

The Soldier is a starter ground tower that fires in bursts. It can be bought for 350 in the store.

The Soldier fires in bursts, with a short delay between each burst. It is a fairly cheap tower, making it recommended for use in earlier waves. It gains hidden detection at Level 2 and flying detection at Level 4 (Level 0 for PVP), so it is an effective counter against Hidden and Flying enemies.

The Soldier is considered an early to mid-game tower. Later on during the game, its effectiveness dwindles against high health enemies, being outclassed by other towers with higher DPS.

Strategy

  • The Soldier is an effective early-game tower at Level 2, having high DPS for a cheap price.
    • By mid-game, you should have multiple Level 3–4 Soldiers, as they can effectively kill enemies that appear there, such as the Dark Necromancer or Slow Boss.
      • However, it loses value as the waves progress due to its DPS being too low to deal with high health enemies that appear later on during the game.
      • It should not be relied on late-game if you have better towers and can be replaced by towers like the Minigunner or Ranger.
  • The Soldier is able to change targets between bursts if another enemy enters its range.
    • This avoids the need to swap targets from time to time.
  • The Soldier's firerate can be boosted by the Commander.
    • However, the Soldier's cooldown cannot be decreased.
    • However, it is not recommended to prioritize this tower over higher DPS alternatives such as the Minigunner and Turret.

Trivia

  • When the game was in Alpha, and for a while after BETA release, the Soldier's upgrade stats were not displayed on its upgrade description. It was simply referred to as "sans gun".
  • The Soldier's name and function is very loosely based on the Soldier from Tower Battles.
  • The Soldier, along with the Turret, are the first towers to have toys made based on them.
    • They are also the first to have mini-figures made based on them, as they appear in the Roblox Core Pack - Tower Defense Simulator.
  • Prior to the Solar Eclipse Update, the Soldier alongside the Commander used to appear on the triumph screen.
